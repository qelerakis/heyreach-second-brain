## Smart LinkedIn automation for  sales teams

### Predictable meeting engine

One workspace, full visibility. Shared campaigns, shared inbox, team reporting, and clean CRM sync. Your team moves as one — and leadership always knows what's working.

User engagement rates and roles of three team members listed with emails and role tags Admin and Member.

### Automate manual actions

Connection requests, follow-ups, InMails, profile views, post likes — all running on autopilot. Use "If Connected" filter to separate current connections from new ones, and sequence them differently.

LinkedIn workflow showing connection status with steps for messages, replies, and waiting times.

### Unified inbox

Every reply from every sender, in one place. Reply on behalf of teammates, take over any conversation without missing a beat, and send voice notes straight from desktop.

### Keep personal messages private

Share campaign conversations with your team without exposing personal LinkedIn messages. Your reps keep their privacy.

### Native HubSpot integration

Every connection request, message, and reply lands in your CRM as a contact activity. Run signal-based outbound and have real ROI visibility. Verified by HubSpot itself.

Connect HeyReach to Claude. Run multichannel sequences with Instantly, Smartlead, or EmailBison. Build with Clay, N8N, Make, or Zapier. If it's in your stack, HeyReach talks to it.

Sales Navigator, Clay, HubSpot, Apollo, ZoomInfo, CommonRoom — pull leads from any source directly into a campaign.
