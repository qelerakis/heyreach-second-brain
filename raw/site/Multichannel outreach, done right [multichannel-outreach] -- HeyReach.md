## All your doubts, answered

How to build multichannel outreach campaigns?

Building multichannel outreach campaigns combines LinkedIn and email touchpoints to maximize response rates.

Start by connecting your LinkedIn automation tool (HeyReach) with your email platform (Instantly or Smartlead) through native integration. This creates a unified system where LinkedIn actions trigger email sequences automatically.

The most effective approach: warm up prospects on LinkedIn first (connection requests, profile views, post engagement), then follow up via email once they've shown interest. HeyReach's native integrations with Instantly and Smartlead sync lead data in both ways, meaning responses on either channel automatically pause outreach on both platforms. This prevents the awkward scenario of messaging someone on LinkedIn while simultaneously emailing them the same pitch.

How to create multichannel outreach sequences?

Effective multichannel sequences layer LinkedIn and email touchpoints strategically rather than blasting prospects on all channels simultaneously.

Multichannel sequences should be **conditional and progressive**, not linear.

A high-performing structure you can steal looks like this:

1. **Primary touch (Channel A)
‍**Example: LinkedIn connection request via HeyReach.

2. **Conditional follow-up
‍**If accepted → LinkedIn message.If ignored → Email follow-up via Instantly or Smartlead after X days.

3. **Cross-channel reinforcement
‍**Reference the previous touch explicitly: “Sent you a note on LinkedIn—sharing more context here.”

4. **Channel-specific pacing
‍**LinkedIn: lower frequency, higher personalization.
Email: structured follow-ups with value-based CTAs.

5. **Single reply source of truth
‍**Replies from LinkedIn (HeyReach) or email (Instantly/Smartlead) should pause all other steps automatically.With HeyReach + Instantly or Smartlead, you create **channel-native sequences** that are logically connected via lead status, rather than forcing everything into one tool.

How to automate multichannel outreach campaigns?

Automating multichannel outreach requires connecting your LinkedIn and email tools so they act as one system, not two separate campaigns you're manually coordinating.

With HeyReach's native integrations to Instantly and Smartlead, automation works like this:

1. **LinkedIn automation via HeyReach
-** Multi-account rotation
-Daily safety limits
- Centralized inbox for LinkedIn replies

2. **Email automation via Instantly or Smartlead
-** Smart inbox rotation
- Deliverability controls
- Automated follow-ups based on opens/replies

3. **Status-based automation
-** When a lead replies on LinkedIn → pause email sequence
- When a lead replies via email → stop LinkedIn actions

4. **Workflow automation layer (optional)
‍**Use Make or Zapier to:
- Sync reply status between tools
- Enrich leads before next step
- Route booked meetings to CRM

5. **Fail-safes
-** Automatic campaign pauses on replies
- Channel cooldowns to avoid over-touching

HeyReach handles LinkedIn scale and safety, while Instantly or Smartlead handle email volume and deliverability—together enabling fully automated, controlled multichannel outreach without spamming or overlap.
