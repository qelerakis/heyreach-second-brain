## All your doubts, answered

What’s a sender?

A sender (or a seat) is a LinkedIn account that you add to your organization. HeyReach don’t charge you for adding team mates, VAs, clients or users. We just charge you per sender/seat or a bundle of senders (see ‘Agency’ and ‘Unlimited’ pricing plans).

What’s the difference between ‘Growth’ and the two other plans?

The ‘Starter’ plan allows you to connect one or multiple LinkedIn senders. The other two plans are made for agencies who really want to go big or manage a big volume of clients. The ‘Starter’ plan provides a dedicated residential proxy to each of the LinkedIn senders, while the ‘Agency’ and ‘Unlimited’ plans require you to bring your own proxies. Also, the ‘Starter’ plan allows you to test HeyReach for 14 days (no CC required) with 3 LinkedIn accounts.

What if I want to get the ‘Agency’ or ‘Unlimited’ plan, but I don’t have my own proxies?

No worries. Book a call with us, and we’ll figure something out.

Can I whitelabel HeyReach for my agency?

Yes. The 'Agency' and 'Unlimited' plans allow you to have your own domain and branding. With the Agency plan, you get one whitelabel branding included free of charge, while the Unlimited plan offers additional whitelabel brandings across multiple workspaces.

Can I test the ‘Unlimited’ plans for 30 days, free of charge?

Yes. You can request a 30 day free trial for both of the plans. [Request a trial here](https://cal.com/forms/a7479115-ec39-4d96-8929-78c5d63a78f3)

Can I invite unlimited number of clients, teammates and VAs for free?

Yes. HeyReach charges only per LinkedIn senders, not per users. Feel free to onboard your whole organization on HeyReach and manage everything together, completely free of charge.
