# Find active LinkedIn prospects [50% more replies]

Source: https://www.heyreach.io/outbound-playbooks/find-active-linkedin-prospects

Summary: Learn how to segment active LinkedIn accounts and maximize LinkedIn outbound potential.

Lead generation agencies and salespeople often face situations that slow down their LinkedIn outbound operations. These include difficulties in finding prospects' email addresses, encountering invalid emails, or dealing with unresponsive contacts.

LinkedIn outreach combined with a multichannel approach can transform your campaigns, potentially increasing replies by 50%.

This playbook leverages HeyReach and Clay to identify LinkedIn prospects with over 500 connections. We use this as an indicator that the prospect is actively engaged in building their LinkedIn network.

Here's the process:

1. Initiate this workflow for prospects with invalid or missing email addresses
2. Determine if the prospect has more than 500 connections
3. Add qualified prospects to our HeyReach campaigns
