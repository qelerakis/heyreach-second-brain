# He closed a $10k deal with a simple LinkedIn outreach campaign

Source: https://www.heyreach.io/outbound-playbooks/craftgenn-campaign

Summary: What's cool about this campaign is that Deepak kept the flow short, sweet, and sharp.

[Deepak](https://www.linkedin.com/in/deepak2431/) founded [Craftgenn](https://craftgenn.com/), a company that specializes in modernizing legacy systems, building resilient digital capabilities, and implementing AI-driven automation.

Recently, he ran a pure LinkedIn outreach campaign that scored the following results in 4 weeks:

* 12.7% replies (out of 63 messages sent)
* 2 meetings booked after initial chat
* Closed a $10k USD deal for his business

What's cool about this campaign is that Deepak kept the flow short, sweet, and sharp. We're talking using Sales Navigator to build a lead list, import it to HeyReach, and finally sending a 2-step LinkedIn outreach campaign - all without breaking a sweat or your company's budget.

The flow starts in Sales Nav, where Deepak went for the following filters:

* Company headcount
* Specific region as they focused on United States - narrowing down geography like I narrow down pizza toppings (decisively and with purpose)
* Function as they wanted to cover a specific department OR specific job titles
* If the company was hiring for specific roles - because according to Deepak, nothing says "ready to spend money" like a company already in spending mode

It starts off with an "If connection" filter to split existing connections from the ones he was yet to connect with. If they were already connected, message 1 would immediately get sent.

Alternatively, Deepak would tell HeyReach to first send a connection request and the same message would follow if the lead accepts the request.

> Hey {FIRST\_NAME},
>
> Are you currently looking for support in scaling your product development efforts? I'd love to explore if we can help!

If a lead didn't reply, in both cases a follow up message would be sent 24h later.

> {FIRST\_NAME},
>
> We recently helped a startup reduce development (AI-based web apps) and design costs by 60% while speeding up their product launch by 6 weeks.
>
> Curious if something like this could be helpful for your team?

Keeping it simple while getting positive replies.
