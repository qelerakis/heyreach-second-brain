# How a simple campaign achieved a 58% reply rate

Source: https://www.heyreach.io/outbound-playbooks/aidan-buckley

Summary: Let me tell you about our friend Aidan who, without the help of a fancy marketing department or a budget that requires its own zip code, managed to get 295 people to actually talk to him on LinkedIn.

Let me tell you about our friend [Aidan](https://www.linkedin.com/in/aidan-buckley-outreach/) who, without the help of a fancy marketing department or a budget that requires its own zip code, managed to get 295 people to actually talk to him on LinkedIn.

This strategy involved just two components:

1. Finding the right people
2. Not immediately trying to sell them something (a strategy I wish the guy at my local electronics store would adopt)

The results were bonkers:

LinkedIn outreach campaign

For targeting, Aidan simply found 5-6 posts about AI SEO, exported the people who engaged with them, and dumped them into HeyReach.

His campaign was refreshingly uncomplicated:

LinkedIn outreach campaign in HeyReach

Step 1: Like someone's post, then wait 3 hours

Step 2: Send this connection request with a message attached

> Hey {FIRST\_NAME}, Noticed you've been engaging with a lot of AI & SEO content lately.
>
> Curious how you're thinking about all the changes.
>
> I'm working on something in the space and thought I'd connect. :)

If they accepted, the campaign ends. If not, he viewed their profile and sent this InMail 3 hours later:

> AI's shaking up SEO pretty fast.
>
> Always interested in how others in the space are adapting.

Even this InMail generated 47 replies on its own.

That's it. The key takeaways, for those of us taking notes:

* Aidan's 58% reply rate happened because he actually tried having conversations instead of immediately pitching his leads. He chose to qualify leads through a deeper conversation rather than on their first reply
* Free LinkedIn accounts have message limits, so consider Sales Navigator if you're planning to streamline this operation
* Targeting matters because you need something in common to talk about. Otherwise, the conversational approach is not going to work

Congratulations Aidan, you crushed it!
