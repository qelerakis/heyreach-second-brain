First move you ought to make is to pick one persona that has one specific problem and then figure out the ideal lead magnet for them.

Tim has a very strict rule when creating lead magnets.

According to him, lead magnet is not a brochure, one pager, or a deck fixing a big problem. Quite the contrary, it is a small niche problem-solver, but they will still need you to handhold them through the rest of it.

In other words, you solve a specific problem your target audience is having while introducing the core problem solved ideally with your product or service. It has to be targeted at people that are problem-aware or solution-aware. For example:

* Problem 1: Hiring talent in the US is expensive
* Solution 1: A recruitment agency that places talent
* Lead magnet 1: Profiles of MPCs that fit the job criteria (remote) + a calculator to calculate savings
* Where to find people: Follow recruitment companies on LinkedIn

Here's another example:

* Problem 2: Landing in the inbox is hard
* Solution 2: Buy domains in seconds
* Lead magnet 2: Video on how to buy domains in under 5 mins without manually setting up DKIM and DNS records
* Where to find people: Follow Smartlead or Instantly on LinkedIn

Step 2 is to prep your LinkedIn outreach campaign. Here’s how that might look like:

lead magnet outreach strategy

Start off with sending a connection request and the add four messages in the mix.

**Message 1:**

> Hi [NAME], I hate to be that guy, but I have this crazy [LEAD MAGNET NAME] that
>
> Can I send it over?
>
> Simply reply "yes" and I will rush it to you.

**Message 2:**

> [NAME], my bad, I couldn't hold back...
>
> Here's the [LEAD MAGNET TYPE]:
>
> Hope you enjoy.

**Message 3:**

> [NAME], seen it? ^^

**Message 4:**

> [NAME], if you liked the last guide, you would love this one:

As you can see, it’s pretty friendly and human. 🙂 So high reply rates are no surprise.

Another thing that’s important to outline here is the fact Tim has 4 follow-up messages. Why? Because life happens. Sometimes people don’t respond because they opened your message while they were on a Zoom call and somebody asked them a question while they were reading your message. We’ve all been there, right? 😛
