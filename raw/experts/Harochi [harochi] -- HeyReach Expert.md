# Harochi

Source: https://www.heyreach.io/experts/harochi

Summary: Harochi builds GTM engines for scaleups and enterprises by unifying data across outbound, inbound, CRM, and marketing.

Harochi builds go-to-market systems for ambitious scaleups and large enterprises that want consistent growth and long-term scale.
Rather than treating outbound, inbound, CRM, and marketing as separate functions, Harochi brings them together into one connected operating system.
By linking every customer touchpoint, teams gain clear visibility into how revenue is actually created.
Marketing can see which initiatives drive real results, sales can focus on the accounts that matter most, and leadership operates from a shared, reliable data foundation.The outcome is a focused GTM setup that removes waste, speeds up pipeline creation, and increases revenue.
With Harochi, companies do not just run campaigns. They build durable systems that improve performance across every channel over time.
