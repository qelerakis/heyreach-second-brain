# How to integrate HeyReach with Clay?

Source: https://www.heyreach.io/blog/clay-integration

Summary: Learn how to integrate Clay with HeyReach to automate your LinkedIn campaigns. Step-by-step setup, personalization tips, and real use cases.

Ever wished your [LinkedIn outreach](https://www.heyreach.io/blog/linkedin-outreach) could scale itself while staying personalized and sharp?

Clay and HeyReach are your GTM tools for streamlining that activity.

These tools are fully integrated to give your outbound a serious upgrade.

With this setup, you can automatically send leads from your Clay tables directly into a HeyReach campaign. That means no more copy-pasting, CSV/ Google Sheets uploads, or juggling accounts. Just pure, seamless automation.

Let’s walk through how to set this up.

### **The building blocks: What’s what?**

Before we dive in, here’s what each tool does:

[**HeyReach**](https://www.heyreach.io/blog/heyreach-review) – Your core LinkedIn automation engine. It manages connection requests, follow-ups, and keeps everything running smoothly across multiple LinkedIn accounts—safely and in the cloud.

**Clay** – Your flexible no-code ai-powered data enrichment tool. It pulls info from hundreds of different data providers and lets you build powerful workflows, and now, can push those leads into HeyReach like a charm.

Got it? Cool. Let’s get into it.

Ready? Let’s connect the two.👇

### **Step 1: connect HeyReach with Clay**

1. Open your Clay table with the leads you want to send.
2. Add a new column > click **Add enrichment** > search for **HeyReach**.
   Select **HeyReach > Add Lead to Campaign**.

When prompted:

* Click **Manage accounts > Add connection**.
* Name your connection (e.g. HeyReachWorkspace1).

Now, jump into HeyReach:

* Go to **Integrations > HeyReach API**.
* [Generate your API key](https://app.heyreach.io/app/integrations/public-api/api) specifically for Clay.
* Paste it into Clay’s connection window.

Boom. You’re connected.

### **Step 2: create your campaign in HeyReach**

Now you need somewhere to send the leads.

1. In HeyReach, go to **Campaigns** > create a new one.
2. Click **Create empty list** > give it a name > mark it as a lead list.
3. Choose your LinkedIn senders and start building your sequence.

Here’s the fun part: **personalization variables**.

Use dynamic variables like this:

{complimentToTheProfile}

{websiteSummary}

{AI\_Icebreaker\_1}

Just make sure:

* No spaces
* Use only letters, numbers, \_ and -
* Keep it consistent across Clay and HeyReach

This is where your Clay data turns into real, meaningful messages.

### **Step 3: push the leads from Clay**

Back in Clay:

1. Select your HeyReach campaign from the dropdown.
2. Map fields like first name, last name, LinkedIn URL, etc.
3. Map **custom fields** exactly as you wrote them in HeyReach {like\_this}.

> ⚠️ Heads up: If you leave the LinkedIn account blank, HeyReach will randomly assign a sender from that campaign.

Once everything is mapped, run the column in Clay. Your leads will start flowing into HeyReach like magic.

Bonus: If you have **auto-update** turned on in Clay, it’ll keep sending new leads automatically.

### **Step 4: test & monitor**

Once your leads hit HeyReach:

* Your campaign status will flip from “Finished” to **Ongoing**.
* Check analytics and make sure all leads and variables are there.
* If anything looks off, tweak your campaign and re-sync.

And just like that—you’ve got a living, breathing lead pipeline running between Clay and HeyReach.
