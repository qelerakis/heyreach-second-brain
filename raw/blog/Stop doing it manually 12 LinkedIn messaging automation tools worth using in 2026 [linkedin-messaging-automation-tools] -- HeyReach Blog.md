# Stop doing it manually: 12 LinkedIn messaging automation tools worth using in 2026

Source: https://www.heyreach.io/blog/linkedin-messaging-automation-tools

Summary: The 12 best LinkedIn messaging automation tools in 2026, compared by features, pricing, and who they're built for.

Manual LinkedIn outreach has a ceiling and it's frustratingly low. Daily limits slow you down, follow-ups fall through the cracks, and juggling multiple accounts turns into a part-time job.

[LinkedIn messaging automation](https://www.heyreach.io/blog/linkedin-message-automation) tools remove that ceiling. They handle the repetitive legwork, such as connection requests, follow-up sequences, reply tracking, inbox organization, so your time goes toward conversations that actually move deals forward.

I went deep on dozens of options and narrowed it down to the 12 [best performing options on the market](https://www.heyreach.io/comparisons). Here's what made the cut and why:

## 1. Expandi

[**Expandi**](https://www.heyreach.io/blog/expandi-review) **is built for sales teams that need outreach sequences to adapt based on how a prospect responds.** If they ignore a connection request, the campaign automatically switches to InMail instead. It's cloud-based, pairs LinkedIn with email, and handles the safety side so your account stays within limits.

**Key features**

* **Inbox and Global Smart Inbox.** Expandi has two inboxes. The first shows your normal LinkedIn messages, where you can reply, snooze, or archive. The Global Smart Inbox collects conversations across different workspaces and lets you filter by labels, tags, notes, read/unread status, and connection type. Note: unlike HeyReach, you can't reply on someone else's behalf, which limits teamwork for agencies.
* **Smart campaigns.** You can build "if-then" sequences that change based on how a lead responds. For example, if they accept your connection request, the system sends a follow-up message. If they ignore it, Expandi can send an InMail instead. The Smart Campaign Builder supports up to 9 actions and 8 conditions in a single sequence, making outreach feel more natural and adaptive. You can also run A/B testing to find which message templates get the best response rates.
* **Image, GIF, and video personalization.** Expandi integrates with Hyperise and Sendspark to let you add dynamic images, GIFs, or personalized videos to your LinkedIn messages. This can significantly boost engagement, but it's a paid add-on on top of your subscription, and the setup requires copying API tokens, which isn't beginner-friendly.
* **Email outreach.** Besides LinkedIn, you can include automated email steps in your sequences. For example, if someone doesn't reply on LinkedIn, Expandi can automatically send a follow-up email. It supports Gmail, Outlook, Yahoo, and other providers, and includes email warm-up to help avoid spam filters.
* **Lead generation tools.** You can import leads from LinkedIn Sales Navigator searches, 1st-degree connections, group members, event attendees, or via CSV files. Expandi can also collect prospect data from LinkedIn posts — for example, scraping everyone who liked or commented on a specific post, so you can target a highly relevant audience. The Mobile Connector campaign type lets you send additional connection requests beyond the standard weekly limit.
* **Workspaces.** Expandi now has a dedicated Workspaces feature for teams and agencies. You can split campaigns by client or team member, set custom roles and permissions, share reports with clients, and manage everything from a centralized dashboard. White-label is available for agencies on the Agency plan (10+ seats).
* **Campaign priority management.** If you run multiple campaigns at once, you can now prioritize which ones Expandi focuses on first, useful when you want to push a time-sensitive campaign ahead of others.
* **Account safety.** Expandi assigns a dedicated country-based IP address to each account and includes profile auto warm-up, smart daily limits, and randomized delays to mimic human behavior. A duplication prevention feature also automatically avoids sending to the same person twice across campaigns.

**Pros:**

* Cloud-based, safer than Chrome extension tools
* Combines [LinkedIn outreach](https://www.heyreach.io/blog/linkedin-outreach) with email outreach in one sequence
* Smart sequences adapt based on how prospects respond
* A/B testing helps you find the best-performing messages
* Image, GIF, and video personalization available
* The workspaces feature makes team and agency management cleaner
* Campaign prioritization helps you focus on what's working
* 14-day free trial with access to all features

**Cons:**

* Some users still report LinkedIn account restrictions if limits aren't set carefully
* At $99/user/month, costs add up fast for larger teams
* You can't reply on behalf of teammates — limits agency use
* Can be confusing for beginners; steeper learning curve
* Image/video personalization and some integrations are paid add-ons, not included in the base price
* Agency pricing (10+ seats) requires booking a call; no public rate

**Pricing:**

* **Business – $99/user/month** Includes unlimited campaigns, LinkedIn + email outreach, Smart Campaign Builder, personalization, A/B testing, analytics, integrations, and all account safety features. **The 14-day free trial gives you access to all features, no credit card required.**
* **Agency – custom pricing (10+ seats)** Includes everything in Business, plus flexible role and permission management, centralized campaign management, transparent client reports, easy template sharing, a dedicated Customer Success Manager, and white-label options. Requires a call to get a quote.

**Best For:** Sales teams needing smart multi-channel sequences with if-then logic

## 2. HeyReach

[**HeyReach**](https://www.heyreach.io/blog/heyreach-review) **is one of the best** [**LinkedIn automation tools**](https://www.heyreach.io/blog/best-linkedin-automation-tools) **for agencies available.** It helps you send more messages and manage more accounts, all without getting blocked by LinkedIn. Instead of juggling multiple logins and missing replies, you get one clean dashboard where everything is organized.

You can send connection requests, LinkedIn messages, and InMails, all while staying within LinkedIn's limits. HeyReach is trusted by 5,500+ companies and rated 4.7 on G2, where it was named a 2026 Fastest Growing Product.

I recommend it for beginners, as well as for [LinkedIn outreach agencie](https://www.heyreach.io/blog/linkedin-outreach-agency)s or sales teams that manage many accounts. And because it's cloud-based, you don't need a risky Chrome extension running in the background.

No matter if you're running two accounts or 20, all conversations show up in one screen. You can reply, add tags, assign leads, and use canned templates for quick follow-ups. With one click, HeyReach's Unibox lets you sync full conversations into your CRM, including HubSpot, Pipedrive, Close, or GoHighLevel, so you never lose context when managing leads. You can also send voice notes directly from your desktop and reply on behalf of your colleagues.
* [**Multi-sender account rotation**](https://help.heyreach.io/en/articles/9897768-multiple-linkedin-senders-on-one-campaign-sender-rotation)**.** LinkedIn limits how many connection requests each account can send per day. HeyReach solves this by rotating multiple accounts inside the same campaign, so you can [scale your outreach](https://www.heyreach.io/blog/how-to-get-clients) safely. Instead of one account sending 20–40 invites a day, five accounts can split that load, reaching far more people without breaking any rules.
* [**Workspaces**](https://help.heyreach.io/en/collections/14572763-workspaces)**.** HeyReach lets you split work by team or client. If you run an agency, each client gets their own space with accounts, leads, and campaigns. Managers can use Master View to watch progress in real time. This keeps things organized and avoids mixing data.
* **Campaign rescheduling.** Sometimes you need to pause or shift your follow-up messages, for example, to avoid weekends or to react to a market change. HeyReach makes it easy to stop, resume, or reschedule campaigns with one click. It keeps your outreach feeling human instead of robotic.
* **Withdraw connection.** Not every connection request gets accepted. With HeyReach, you can automatically pull back old, unanswered invites. This keeps your profile clean and improves your acceptance rate over time.

* **Targeted filters & tags.** Filters and tags let you group conversations by sender, campaign, or response type. For example, tag leads as "generic reply" and drop them into a re-engagement sequence later. No more scrolling forever when you're managing hundreds of leads.
* **Sequences for beginners.** HeyReach gives you [drag-and-drop message sequences](https://www.heyreach.io/blog/linkedin-message-automation) that are easy to build: view profile → send connection request → send message → send follow-up. You can start with built-in templates and personalize from there. You also get "If Connected" logic, which automatically separates your existing connections from new ones, so your messages always feel relevant.
* **Privacy controls.** If you connect your personal account but don't want all chats imported, you can choose to track only conversations started through HeyReach. Great for founders or executives who want privacy while still using automation.
* **Analytics & reports.** HeyReach tracks what matters: requests sent and accepted, messages sent and replied to, InMail stats, A/B test results, and individual sender performance. You can export data to CSV for deeper analysis.
* [**Integrations**](https://www.heyreach.io/integrations)**.** HeyReach connects natively with [Clay](https://www.heyreach.io/integration/clay), [RB2B](https://www.heyreach.io/integration/rb2b), [Trigify](https://www.heyreach.io/integration/trigify), and more. This lets you build multi-channel workflows that integrate LinkedIn and email. The native Instantly and Smartlead integrations make multichannel outreach straightforward, no developer needed.
* **Sales Navigator / InMail support.** If you have a Sales Navigator or Recruiter subscription, you can add InMails to your outreach and reach people even if they haven't accepted your connection request yet.
* Find Email (lead enrichment). HeyReach now includes a built-in "Find Email" step that lets you find verified email addresses directly inside the platform — no third-party tool needed. Each subscription includes a starting credit allocation, and you can top up anytime.

**Pros:**

* You can safely reach more people by rotating across multiple LinkedIn accounts
* One Unibox means you never miss a reply, even across 10+ accounts
* Easy CRM sync with HubSpot, Pipedrive, and others
* Cloud-based, no Chrome extension needed
* MCP server lets you connect with AI tools for fully automated workflows
* Native multichannel support via Instantly and Smartlead integrations
* Built-in email finder removes the need for extra enrichment tools

* Messages don't send instantly; there can be a short delay

* MCP is powerful but may feel complex for complete beginners
* Agency and Unlimited plans require you to bring your own proxies (or arrange a custom solution with HeyReach)

**Pricing:**

Includes all LinkedIn actions, unlimited campaigns, Unified Inbox, MCP server, integrations, workspaces, and a starting email credit allocation. Comes with a free 14-day trial (no credit card required).

## 3. Meet Alfred

[**Meet Alfred**](https://www.heyreach.io/blog/meet-alfred-review) **is a LinkedIn automation tool for teams that want LinkedIn, email, and X outreach managed from one place, without needing a separate CRM.**

 It helps you send connection requests, LinkedIn messages, and follow-up messages without the manual grind. You can build simple templates, run outreach campaigns, and see campaign performance in one place. It also supports multi-channel outreach (LinkedIn, email, and X (Twitter)) and has a built-in CRM to keep your lead lists tidy.

Meet Alfred has been around since 2016, making it one of the original LinkedIn automation tools on the market. It's trusted by over 150,000 registered users from 92 countries, and rated 4.7 out of 5 on Trustpilot.

**Key features**

* **LinkedIn outreach.** You can send connection requests, InMails, and follow-up messages automatically. To keep things natural, Alfred can first visit profiles or like LinkedIn posts before reaching out, so your actions look more human and boost response rates.
* **Sequences & templates.** Build step-by-step workflows with delays between each message. Use ready-made message templates to keep your tone consistent. If someone replies, the sequence pauses automatically to avoid spamming. Alfred also includes a library of 600+ templates to help you get started quickly.
* **Inbox (per account).** All messages from one LinkedIn account show up in one place. You can reply, tag, and add notes without switching screens. On the side, you'll see the lead's LinkedIn profile details. Note: there's no unified inbox across multiple accounts, which makes team use harder.
* **Built-in CRM.** Keep all your leads organized with tags and filters, like job title, company, or location. You can bulk-edit lead lists to save time. The advanced CRM (available on Pro and Team plans) also lets you auto-withdraw old pending connection requests and track analytics for withdrawn invitations.
* **Lead import.** Add prospects from LinkedIn Sales Navigator, regular LinkedIn search, or by uploading a CSV file. Alfred checks for duplicates and cleans the list for you. The platform also has a built-in Lead Finder tool that uses AI to search the web for leads based on your ideal customer profile, giving you up to 10,000 results depending on your plan.
* **Integrations.** Sync your data with HubSpot, Salesforce, or Pipedrive. Use Zapier or webhooks to connect other tools so your CRM records stay up to date. Meet Alfred also integrates with Facebook and Instagram for scheduling social media posts.
* **Reporting.** Track invites sent and accepted, messages, replies, and overall campaign performance. Team plans also allow you to export team-level analytics and campaign data for deeper review.
* AI personalization. Meet Alfred includes AI-powered personalization features that let you tailor messages at scale. You can set custom personalization snippets and adjust AI settings to control how your outreach sounds, keeping it relevant without writing every message from scratch.
* Social media posting. Beyond outreach, Meet Alfred lets you schedule and publish posts on LinkedIn, Facebook, Twitter/X, and Instagram directly from the platform, which is handy if you want to stay active on social while your campaigns run in the background.

**Pros:**

* Simple cloud-based setup; no Chrome extension needed
* Clear templates and workflows — good for beginners
* Built-in CRM means you may not need a separate tool
* Solid integrations: Zapier, webhooks, HubSpot, Salesforce, Pipedrive
* Multi-channel outreach across LinkedIn, email, and X (Twitter)
* AI personalization and 600+ message templates included
* Built-in Lead Finder, no need for a separate prospecting tool on higher plans
* Social media scheduling is included on Pro and Team plans

**Cons:**

* No true unified inbox; you can't reply on behalf of teammates across accounts
* UI can feel clunky; not the most intuitive experience
* Reports are high-level; fewer deep metrics compared to some competitors
* Plans start at $59/user/month (monthly billing)
* Aggressive automation settings can risk LinkedIn flags or account bans if not used carefully

**Pricing:**

* **Pro – $99/user/month***.* The most popular plan. Includes everything in Basic, plus unlimited campaigns, Sales Navigator support, multi-channel automation (LinkedIn + email + X), advanced CRM, InMail automation, LinkedIn Groups and Events campaigns, social media post scheduling, Zapier/webhooks integrations, and data export.

## 4. LinkedHelper

[**LinkedHelper**](https://www.heyreach.io/blog/linked-helper-review) **is the go-to for solo users and consultants who want detailed drip control at the lowest price point on the market.** It automates connection requests, messages, and follow-ups, and comes with a built-in CRM, with no extra cost.

Unlike modern cloud-based tools, Linked Helper runs as a desktop app, which can feel heavier and less smooth for beginners. It is rated 4.8 on Capterra, 4.5 on G2, and 4.9 on Trustpilot, and is trusted by over 10,000 businesses worldwide.

⚠️ Note: Linked Helper promotes templates, variables, and drip logic. It now includes a built-in AI message generator to help you write and personalize sequences, but it does not have a full native AI assistant for composing messages from scratch.

**Key features**

* **LinkedIn outreach.** You can run outreach campaigns with pre-made templates. For example: send an invite → cancel it if ignored after 30 days → send a welcome message once accepted. Everything is automated but still customizable with variables like name, company, or job title. Linked Helper supports over 30 automated LinkedIn actions in total, including connection requests, InMails, messages to 1st connections, profile follows, skill endorsements, post likes and comments, group invites, event invites, and more.
* [**Drip campaigns**](https://www.heyreach.io/blog/drip-campaigns)**.** Linked Helper lets you set up drip campaigns, sequences of actions that run step by step. For example: view a profile → send invite → send message → send follow-up. You control the delays between each step so the flow feels natural. You can also use IF-THEN-ELSE logic and Spintax (automatic message variation) so no two messages look exactly the same, which helps protect your account.
* **Built-in CRM.** Unlike many tools, Linked Helper has a CRM built right into the app. You can tag leads, filter them, and move them between campaigns. It tracks replies so you don't send the wrong follow-up. You can export everything to CSV for use in a bigger CRM like HubSpot or Salesforce. The CRM also lets you add custom fields and view the full history of automated interactions with each lead.
* **Inbox.** Linked Helper has a built-in inbox that collects replies from your campaigns and lets you reply directly, without switching to LinkedIn. You can also scrape and export your full messaging history to CSV, or route it to your CRM via Zapier or webhooks. That said, it doesn't feel like a true unified inbox, and conversations are still easier to manage directly in LinkedIn.
* **Email finder.** Linked Helper includes an email finder that can uncover verified emails, **direct phone numbers**, and profile data for 2nd and 3rd-degree connections. Credits are included in both plans: 620 for Standard and 3,100 for Pro, and you can buy more starting at $15 per 1,000 credits.
* Integrations. Linked Helper now integrates directly with HubSpot, Salesforce, Pipedrive, Zoho CRM & Recruit, ActiveCampaign, Instantly, Streak, Close, Capsule, HighLevel, Google Sheets, Make, Zapier, and more. These help you sync campaign data and lead information into your broader workflows automatically.
* Audience engagement tools. Beyond outreach, Linked Helper can automatically like and comment on posts, endorse skills, invite connections to LinkedIn groups or events, and even tag selected leads under posts using the "Boost post" feature, a useful way to warm up an audience before connecting.

**Pros:**

* Automates connection requests, LinkedIn messages, and 30+ other LinkedIn actions
* Includes a built-in CRM at no extra cost
* Can run detailed drip campaigns with many steps and IF-THEN-ELSE logic
* Expanded integrations: now includes ActiveCampaign, Instantly, HighLevel, Make, and more
* Very affordable pricing compared to most tools
* Rated 4.9 on Trustpilot with 24/7 support and response within 20 minutes

**Cons:**

* No proper unified inbox; managing replies is harder than with dedicated tools
* Runs as a desktop app, prospecting stops when you close the program
* Can [slow down your computer](https://mackeeper.com/blog/mac-is-running-slow/) while running
* Steeper learning curve for new users
* Limited team features, no multi-account sender rotation like HeyReach

**Pricing:**

* **Free trial – 14 days** (no credit card required, all features included)
* **Standard – $15/month** *(or as low as $8.25/month on annual billing).* Covers essential LinkedIn automation, messages, data scraping, and 620 email finder credits.
* **Pro – $45/month** *(or as low as $24.75/month on annual billing).* Unlocks advanced lead generation, more CRM options, message scraping, and 3,100 email finder credits.

*Note: one license = one LinkedIn account. You can switch between accounts, but you cannot run two at the same time.*
