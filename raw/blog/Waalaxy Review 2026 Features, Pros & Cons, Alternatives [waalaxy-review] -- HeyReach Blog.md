## Waalaxy at a glance

Waalaxy is a great solution for people who want to optimize and scale their LinkedIn outreach.

This tool helps you to automate tasks — it literally works like a prospecting tool that let’s you automate LinkedIn connection and outreach, while making sure all LinkedIn rules are followed.

At first glance, even for beginners, Waalaxy has surprisingly intuitive interface. The platform is easy to navigate, but catching up with all new features and functions can take some time.

When you create an account on Waalaxy, on home page you will notice a few simple steps to complete to get quick access to the dashboard.

For users who still need assistance, there is a short video tutorial and a 27/7 Live AI chat option (Unfortunately, if you need agent assistance, you will get an answer within 24 hours).

Waalaxy software is designed to break [LinkedIn lead generation](https://www.heyreach.io/blog/how-to-use-linkedin-sales-navigator) processes into three stages:

‍**First stage**: You have to select potential connections based on what you are looking for. Define keywords based on specific criteria.

**Second stage**: Set up a campaign and write tailored messages. Be creative and avoid ChatGPT-generated content. Implement LinkedIn as well as email outreach.

‍**Third stage**: Last but not least, you can sit down and relax. Waalaxy will take it from here and automatize the whole process for you.

Before we move to features of Waalaxy’s software, let’s take a moment to review user feedback and customer satisfaction from reputable sources.

According to [Capterra](https://www.capterra.com/p/230606/Waalaxy/reviews/), positive Waalaxy reviews highlight their user-friendly interface and great automatization features. On the other side, there are some major concerns between users regarding pricing competitiveness and the inability to scale campaigns.

### Pros

* LinkedIn message automation
* Go multichannel and add email outreach
* Email finder and enrichment capabilities
* Native integrations available

### Cons

* Quite expensive
* Inability to scale campaigns
* No option to create custom campaigns, you can only select from their pre-built templates
* Although they are a cloud-based solution, the initial login is through an extension which increases your risk of being flagged
* Late inquiry response from support

## Waalaxy feature overview

We can agree for sure that Waalaxy is ideal for two types of use cases. Whether you want to expand your Linkedin network or utilize email outreach strategy, Waalaxy offers versatile tools and features that will make your outreach campaign more efficient.

Let’s explore all the features this outreach software has to offer.

### 1. LinkedIn outreach

Before we dive into details, let’s start from the beginning.

After you create an account on Waalaxy, you will have to install a Chrome extension. On the main dashboard, you will notice the blue ‘’**Start Campaign**’’ button.

Click on the ‘’Start Campaign’’ button, and then select ‘’**Browse Sequences**

If you don’t know which [outreach strategy](https://www.heyreach.io/blog/outreach-strategies) to use, you can choose from the pre-made outreach templates Waalaxy has to offer.

The next thing is to generate or import a list of prospects from your LinkedIn account using the Waalaxy Chrome extension you installed.

The best Waalaxy feature is LinkedIn outreach. It gives you the possibility to specify lead search so you can target [connection requests](https://www.heyreach.io/blog/linkedin-connection-message) according to your current preferences and career goals. Set up the list name and the number of prospects you want to import.

It's important to mention that you can customize notes when sending connection requests so you can increase the chance of a response.

Now, it’s time to finish campaign creation and schedule it. If you are all set up and satisfied with everything, click the ‘’**Launch my campaign**’’ button and you're set for success.

As I'm trying to build relationships and not go in guns blazing with my sales approach, I'm missing a few LinkedIn actions in the Waalaxy software.

While Waalaxy gives me the ability to send personalized messages, automate connection invitations, and visit a prospect’s profile, HeyReach enhances my experience with additional features, such as following a prospect’s profile and liking a post.

### 2. Adding multiple senders

Another cool Waalaxy feature is the option to easily add team members. This feature is designed for agencies, companies, sales teams, or recruiters. They offer some quality assets such as:

* Different roles for teammates you can add by inviting them to join your team
* It’s easy to import leads from one account to another
* Sync CRM and export data to your favorite tools
* Monitor the team’s performance and results in a centralized dashboard
* Option to activate anti-duplicate to avoid spamming and contacting the same leads twice

Waalaxy Team is available for all plans, and you can choose 1 month, 3 month, or 12-month plan. Price is determined according to the number of team members and subscription duration.

While we think Waalaxy Team provides some quite amazing features, it’s important to note their major drawback for some users. Their pricing.

When we compare Waalaxy’s Team yearly plan to the yearly HeyReach team package on the other side, we notice that HeyReach offers much more affordable plans, especially for agencies, enabling them to scale their outreach effectively.

### 3. Reporting and unified inbox

Waalaxy reporting system is clean and user-friendly, we have to say that the visual presentation of data is quite impressive. They provide comprehensive and visually appealing insight into your outreach campaign.

You can track key metrics like acceptance and response rates to **measure** [**campaign performance.**](https://www.heyreach.io/blog/campaign-performance)

Waalaxy also offers a smart inbox, but access to a unified inbox requires paid add-ons. In contrast, HeyReach includes a smart inbox in its base plan, **with no hidden costs.**

Another limitation of Waalaxy’s smart inbox is that you can’t reply on behalf of teammates, which can slow down collaboration. **HeyReach supports team-based replies,** making outreach faster and more efficient for collaborative teams.

### 4. Email outreach and email finder

Great addition thing with Waalaxy’ is that with them you can go from LinkedIn to multi-channel [prospecting](https://www.heyreach.io/blog/prospecting-tactics) quite fast.

Waalaxy’s Email outreach is a feature similar to their LinkedIn outreach feature.

💡However, note that this option is available only for users with a business plan tier. Email campaigns are not and option on free trial nor advanced plan.

Let’s set up an email finder and outreach campaign together.

Firstly, choose the outreach plan in the ‘’Select a sequence’’ part.

Now choose from where you want to import or generate new prospects, and according to your goal for this campaign, write a personalized email.

Waalaxy applies limits based on the plan you choose. The Pro plan allows 300 LinkedIn invitations per month, while Advanced, Business, and Enterprise plans increase this to 800 invitations per month, with Advanced adding extra capacity and priority support.

Email outreach is only available on the Business plan and above, making Waalaxy better suited for smaller or mid-scale LinkedIn-first campaigns **unless you upgrade.** While higher-tier plans unlock more features and collaboration options, scaling outreach still depends heavily on plan limits.

Overall, Waalaxy is a solid choice for teams starting with LinkedIn outreach or running structured, volume-controlled campaigns, especially if you’re comfortable **scaling gradually through higher plans.**

## Waalaxy pricing plans

* **Pro – €39/user/mo:** Unlimited campaigns, 300 LinkedIn invitations/month, automated [follow-ups,](https://www.heyreach.io/blog/sales-follow-up) smart reply detection, CRM sync (2,000+ tools), CSV imports/exports, LinkedIn imports, duplicate prevention, basic team collaboration, email finder credits, 48h support.
* **Advanced – €99/user/mo:** Everything in Pro, 800 LinkedIn invitations/month, additional invitation capacity, live chat support, built for safely scaling LinkedIn outreach.
* **Business – €139/user/mo:** Everything in Advanced, LinkedIn + email outreach, cold email sequences, automated email follow-ups, [multiple sender accounts,](https://www.heyreach.io/blog/manage-multiple-linkedin-accounts) higher email finder credits, priority support.
* **Enterprise – Custom pricing (5+ seats):** Chosen plan features plus team workspace, unified billing, volume discounts, team analytics dashboard, multi-account imports, anti-duplicate security, dedicated onboarding, and scalable team management.
