# HubSpot integration is live: sync outreach activity automatically

Source: https://www.heyreach.io/blog/hubspot-integration-is-live

Summary: HeyReach now syncs natively with HubSpot. Log connection requests, messages, and replies as CRM activities — automatically. Approved by HubSpot.

LinkedIn outbound is one of your highest-performing channels. It's also the only one that lives entirely outside your CRM.

That changes today.

Connection requests, messages, and replies sit in a separate tool with no record of previous activity. When a rep leaves, that history walks out the door. When leadership asks how LinkedIn is contributing to pipeline, there's no answer in HubSpot.

Today we're launching native HubSpot integration — bringing every LinkedIn touchpoint your team makes directly into the contacts you already have in your CRM. Approved and verified by our partners at HubSpot.

## Launch campaigns straight from HubSpot lists

Pull any HubSpot contact list — static or dynamic — directly into HeyReach as a campaign source.

HeyReach reads the HubSpot field you select for each contact's LinkedIn URL. No URL? Contacts get enriched through reverse email lookup directly in HeyReach. Done in seconds.

## Every LinkedIn action becomes a HubSpot activity

From the moment your campaign runs, every action your team takes inside HeyReach is logged as a native activity on the matching HubSpot contact:

* Connection requests sent and accepted
* Messages sent and replied
* InMails sent and replied
* Campaign enrolments
* List assignments

Conversations are stored as notes on the contact — one per sender — so the full LinkedIn thread sits right next to the email thread. When a rep leaves, the conversation history stays.

## Built for admins who own the data

You control everything.

Sync triggers let you decide when contacts update — on first reply (default and recommended) or when added to a HeyReach campaign. Activity logging lets you choose which actions get recorded and which stay quiet.

On install, a dedicated HeyReach property group is created in HubSpot with 20 properties covering:

* Campaign attribution
* First and last touch dates
* LinkedIn Sender
* Reply sentiment — positive, neutral, or negative classification used for auto-tagging warm leads
* Engagement metrics

All of it ready to filter, segment, and build views against.

LinkedIn becomes a tracked layer on your existing contacts. The outbound history stays. The contribution shows up where leadership already looks.
