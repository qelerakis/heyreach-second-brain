## **HeyReach at a glance**

### **HeyReach and safety**

First thing first, you need to be thinking about the safety of your (and your clients’!) [LinkedIn accounts](https://www.heyreach.io/blog/linkedin-account-types).

HeyReach is your biggest ally here, as the tool has built-in hard daily limits on LinkedIn actions, per account, that are adjusted to LinkedIn limits.

Yes, we all know there’s no official numbers, and that there’s a lot of theory about how daily limits can fluctuate from account to account  – but at HeyReach we keep them at 40 actions a day because that’s what we saw in practice is the safest limitation.

You can go below that, but never ever above – the system simply won’t let you even if you tried.

The moment you approach those limits HeyReach will stop your activities as a safety measure.

As an additional safety layer, our customer success team recommends that you don’t send any manual connection requests while campaigns are running in your outreach automation tool of choice, because  tracking and applying limits gets tricky.

You can edit limits for follows, connection requests, messages, inMail messages, profile views, and posts like inside HeyReach for every connected account.

We may be playing safe, but we play a real automation game. Your LinkedIn account safety won’t be disrupted by activities performed by human bots from sweatshops located in some remote place on a random middle-of-nowhere place on the globe.

Thanks to something called ‘proxy server’, all automated activities will be registered as if they’re coming from the original geographical place of your LinkedIn account. Because they do.

This basically means that if you put ‘Chicago, USA’ as your location, we’ll use our proxy servers for Chicago, USA.

If you’re an advanced GTM engineer, or just a geek, you can also [set up your own custom proxy](https://help.heyreach.io/en/articles/9931282-how-to-connect-my-linkedin-account-by-using-a-custom-proxy).

### **MCP Server integrations**

MCP server allows you to connect your tool of choice (in this case HeyReach) with your AI agent of choice (think ChatGPT, Claude, Perplexity, n8n…). Once you integrate them, you can operate from your platform of choice across all of your tools connected with MCP servers using detailed [AI prompts](https://www.heyreach.io/blog/ai-sales-prompts).

This means that, for example, you can connect HeyReach, n8n, Clay, and HubSpot together and unify them with Claude, and then operate from Claude - create campaigns, clean lists, push leads, and attribute them according to their [buying intent signals](https://www.heyreach.io/blog/buyer-intent-signals).

HeyReach is the only LinkedIn automation platform that offers MCP Server type of integration. This is a brand new way of integrating tools, and it's also a revolutionary approach in the outbound ecosystem.
