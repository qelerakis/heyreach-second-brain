## **Meet Alfred at a glance**

Meet Alfred is a user-friendly LinkedIn automation tool designed to improve your outreach, lead generation processes, and sales efforts. It lets you automatically send hundreds of [personalized follow-up messages](https://www.heyreach.io/blog/follow-up-email-and-linkedin-message) daily across LinkedIn, email, and X.

Probably the best thing about it is its ability to create a multi-channel sales pipeline and connect it with most applications and tools through Zapier integration.

The built-in CRM makes managing leads easier, helping you save 10+ hours a week, according to the Meet Alfred team.

### **Pros of using Meet Alfred:**

* Lead filtering and management
* Multichannel outreach—[reach prospects via LinkedIn,](https://www.heyreach.io/blog/prospecting-tactics) email, and X
* Easy-to-follow steps for connecting with potential clients
* Built-in CRM
* Clear and straightforward reports on campaign performance

### **Cons of using Meet Alfred:**

* The interface feels a bit off and might take some time to get used to. For example, when you click something in the toolbar, like campaigns, nothing happens until you select an option that appears below.
* You can’t reply on behalf of other LinkedIn senders in shared campaigns, which can slow things down. This is maybe one of the key advantages HeyReach has over MeetAlfred as it gives you a very [powerful unibox.](https://help.heyreach.io/en/articles/9877943-unibox-screen-overview)‍
* Risk of being banned due to aggressive automation practices

## **Meet Alfred feature overview**

Meet Alfred is built for salespeople, marketers, and recruiters who want to elevate their LinkedIn outreach as a core part of their business plan and strategy.

It helps you set up campaigns to automatically send [connection requests,](https://www.heyreach.io/blog/linkedin-connection-automation-tool) follow-ups, and messages to your target audience on the social media platform of your choice as well as email campaigns.

Let’s break down each feature in detail.

### **Lead generation, filtering & lead management**

I used Meet Alfred to connect with LinkedIn event attendees by importing a CSV file with their profiles.

1. I picked a marketing event that matched my audience.
2. I collected attendee profiles and uploaded them as a CSV to Meet Alfred.
3. The platform removed duplicates and verified profiles automatically.
4. I filtered out profiles without photos or missing details and focused on premium users.
5. I started with light engagement (profile views, follows, likes).
6. Then I sent personalized messages using templates with small custom touches.
7. Messages were scheduled with natural delays to keep everything organic.

Once the campaign went live, I tracked responses, handled follow-ups, and [managed leads.](https://www.heyreach.io/blog/lead-scoring-automation) Overall, the process was pretty straightforward.

### **Multichannel outreach**

Another good thing about Meet Alfred is how seamlessly it lets you create [multi-channel campaigns.](https://www.heyreach.io/blog/multichannel-outreach) For example, I set one up to reach out to project managers, hoping to gather quotes for an article I’m working on.

The process was smooth—after connecting LinkedIn, email, and X, I used LinkedIn Search to target my audience.

And then in the sequence-building step, I created a mix of LinkedIn connection requests, email follow-ups, and X messages for extra reach.

It was important to get the LinkedIn connection right, though, because if they didn’t accept, the rest of the steps wouldn’t trigger.

I also made sure my email was authorized, which is a key step to avoid any issues with sending follow-ups within campaign sequences.

One thing to note is that the X option only works if your lead has an X handle on their profile—something I realized halfway through. Quite a hassle.

### **LinkedIn prospecting and all the steps**

I wanted to connect with marketers for virtual coffee chats to exchange ideas, so I tested Meet Alfred’s prospecting feature.

Here’s how I set it up:

1. First, I clicked **“Create”** and selected **“LinkedIn Campaign.”**
2. Then, I imported a clean list of marketing specialists and decision-makers from **Trigify.**
3. After that, I applied filters to keep my outreach targeted.
4. Next, I added light engagement by viewing profiles, following, and sending connection requests to warm up prospects.
5. For messaging, I wrote short, personalized notes using their name and a friendly tone.
6. I also turned on auto follow-ups, sending a message 15 minutes after a request was accepted.
7. To stay organized, I tagged leads as **“Marketers”** for easier inbox navigation.
8. Finally, I named my campaign **“Virtual coffee with marketers ☕️”** and hit **Publish** to start connecting with new leads.

Since things started happening, I checked out the inbox to see how it worked.

In their streamline inbox, I could reply to messages, use emojis, and attach files — including visuals.

On the right side, I could quickly view the contact’s LinkedIn profile, which is another positive aspect.

However, compared to HeyReach, I’m missing the Unibox feature here, which allows me to reply on all LinkedIn messages from the profiles included in one campaign. No need to switch between accounts, log into each LinkedIn profile, or remind others to check their inboxes. It’s a big time-saver for SDRs or VAs managing multiple [LinkedIn accounts](https://www.heyreach.io/blog/linkedin-account-types) within one team when doubling down on [LinkedIn lead generation.](https://www.heyreach.io/blog/linkedin-lead-generation-strategy)

So it’s definitely something I’d love to see added here.

As I scrolled down, I used the Alfred tags I had created to organize my contacts and add notes for easy reference later.

It was easy to find specific conversations by searching for keywords or names, and I could filter messages by tagged, archived, or unread.

### **Built-in LinkedIn CRM integration**

The CRM has features for managing team members, allowing multiple LinkedIn profiles, assigning roles, and accessing [campaign performance](https://www.heyreach.io/blog/campaign-performance) stats collectively.

It is fully cloud-based, so there's no need to download a desktop app or keep LinkedIn open. Users can set custom working hours for the platform to operate independently.

You can easily filter leads by name, company, job title, location, campaign tags, and whether they're included or excluded from your campaigns.

One feature I found especially handy is the bulk selection tool. It allows you to manage multiple leads at once, which saves a ton of time when you need to add tags or exclude several leads in one go.

#### **Tag Manager**

As I’ve mentioned earlier, Tags in Meet Alfred help you organize and categorize your connections and leads. You can quickly identify specific groups or segments within your contacts.

To access your tags, go to **'CRM' > 'Tag Manager.'** Click **'Create Tag'** to name your tag and select a color; it will appear in your Tag Manager right away.

You can also delete and edit tags whenever you want. After saving, your changes will update all contacts associated with that tag.

#### **Integrations**

Meet Alfred connects with various LinkedIn account types (Basic, Premium, and Sales Navigator), and supports integration with CRM platforms like Salesforce and HubSpot via Zapier or Webhook.

Users can also automate email outreach using Google or Microsoft accounts and manage social media posts across Facebook, Instagram, and X.

#### **Reports**

Meet Alfred’s reports and analytics give clear insights into what’s working and what’s not.

1. Go to **Analytics > Dashboard** to track campaign performance and conversion rates in real time.
2. You can easily sort data by today, yesterday, this week, this month, or custom dates.

Key features include:

* **Campaign stats:** See total campaigns, invitations, connections, profile views, messages, and InMails.
* **Team campaigns:** Track performance across multiple accounts.
* **Segmented overview:** Analyze activity over specific date ranges.**‍**
* **Campaign insights:** Use analytics to understand customer behavior and engagement for better decisions.

## **Meet Alfred pricing**

Meet Alfred has three pricing plans and each of them comes with a free trial (no credit card approach), which is a great way to explore the features without any commitment:

1. **Basic:** $59/user/month for [automated LinkedIn outreach,](https://www.heyreach.io/blog/automated-linkedin-messaging) including 3 active campaigns, basic CRM, smart inbox, message templates, automated greetings, custom tags, and notes.
2. **Pro**: $99/user/month for unlimited campaigns and advanced features like Sales Navigator support, multi-channel automation, advanced CRM, InMail automation, content retargeting, and data export.
3. **Teams**: $79/user/month (minimum 3 users) for larger teams, offering all Pro features plus volume discounts, advanced settings, team inbox management, and priority support.

What I don’t like here is the fact that Meet Alfred’s costs shoot up as you scale, and that per-sender pricing can **eat into your profit margins pretty quickly.**

I think they could have focused more on agencies when putting together their pricing structure, just like HeyReach did.

For instance, [HeyReach offers package](https://www.heyreach.io/pricing) with a flat monthly fee for **unlimited senders,** which is way more budget-friendly for teams to scale their outreach. No need to stress over per-sender fees or hidden add-ons at all.

This predictable, cost-effective setup makes HeyReach a smarter—and safer—choice for [agencies that want to scale efficiently.](https://www.heyreach.io/blog/ai-outreach)
