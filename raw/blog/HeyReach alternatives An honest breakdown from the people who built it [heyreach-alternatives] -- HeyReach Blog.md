## **Five alternatives to HeyReach**

If you are an agency, GTM or Sales team looking to scale, HeyReach is your best bet.

However, if your needs are different, you may want to explore other tools to see if any of them better suit your current needs. We have curated a list of five other tools in the B2B lead generation category and will provide an honest review of each.

The goal is to help you find the tool that meets your needs. Let's take a look at them and what they do.

### **1. Expandi**

Expandi is a LinkedIn automation tool that lets you automate your outreach. It handles repetitive and boring tasks, such as sending connections, LinkedIn messages, and sequences, allowing you to focus more on productive sales efforts.

Unlike other LinkedIn lead generation platforms like Dux-Soup, Linked Helper, or MeetAlfred, Expandi is cloud-based. It runs on its cloud server, not on your computer or in your browser. This gives Expandi an edge because it keeps your campaigns running even when your browser or devices are not running.

Its cloud-based feature also allows it to adapt to LinkedIn's terms of service, keeping to its action limits and increasing the safety of your LinkedIn accounts.

**Pricing:** $99 per seat per month. An agency plan with 10+ seats requires a sales call (custom pricing).

> Check out a detailed [review of Expandi](https://www.heyreach.io/blog/expandi-review) explaining its key features

#### **HeyReach Vs Expandi: a side-by-side comparison**

##### **1. MCP Server:**

HeyReach has recently implemented the [MCP server feature,](https://www.heyreach.io/mcp) which takes it miles ahead of Expandi in functionality

##### **2. Multiple Account Rotation in a Campaign:**

While both Expandi and HeyReach are cloud-based, HeyReach has differentiated itself and taken a step forward in implementing multiple account rotation during campaigns to reduce the risk of banned accounts to the bare minimum.

##### **3. Pricing:**

HeyReach employs a flat pricing model with three plans, offering more flexibility. In contrast, Expandi has two pricing plans, with the second plan requiring a sales call. Besides, you can try HeyReach for free, without inputting your credit card details, while Expandi does.

##### **4. Ease of Use and Navigation:**

HeyReach is easier to navigate compared to Expandi, especially at the onboarding phase.

[Source](https://www.g2.com/products/expandi-io/reviews/expandi-io-review-11505335)

#### **Takeaway**

If you want multi-channel sequences (LinkedIn + Email) and are comfortable with more complexity, Expandi is a HeyReach alternative worth considering.

### **2. Waalaxy**

Waalaxy is another LinkedIn outreach automation tool that combines LinkedIn with email. Together with its intuitive UI, Waalaxy is easy to navigate, as evident in user reviews on the internet. To launch a campaign in Waalaxy, it takes three steps.

1. Filter your connections based on your requirement so that you only get targeted leads.
2. Based on your outreach strategy, set up your campaign and your personalized message for every step.
3. Kickstart your campaign and let Waalaxy do the work for you.

**Pricing (per user/month):**

* Pro: €19/month; 300 invitations/month, unlimited campaigns
* Advanced: €49/month; 800 invitations/month, API access, Make/Zapier/n8n
* Business: €69/month; 800 invitations/month + cold email sequences, multichannel campaigns
* Enterprise: Custom (5+ seats)

> ​Check out the [key features of Waalaxy](https://www.heyreach.io/blog/waalaxy-review) here together with its pros and cons.

#### **HeyReach Vs Waalaxy: a side-by-side comparison**

##### **1. Innovations**

HeyReach is currently the only LinkedIn outreach tool with the MCP server feature, which is a significant advantage over other tools in the category. Same with the multiple account rotation in HeyReach, which reduces the risk of account ban.

HeyReach also offers features such as white-label in the Agency plan that allows you customize the tool with your logo, brand colours etc so that it appears like you built the tool.

##### **2. Cloud-Based**

It is essential to note that Waalaxy is browser-based (operates as a chrome extension), not cloud-based. This means you must keep your laptop on and the tab open before your campaign can run successfully.

HeyReach, on the other hand, can run your campaigns around the clock regardless of whether your laptop is on or not.

##### **3. Scalability**

If you are a large sales team or a lead generation agency seeking scalability, HeyReach would be a better tool for you.

##### **4. Pricing**

Waalaxy charges per user for each plan. This means that if you are a larger team, such as an agency, you would have to pay more as you scale.

HeyReach offers a flat pricing model; regardless of how much you scale, the highest plan available is the unlimited plan, which includes everything you need.

#### **Takeaway**

If you are a solopreneur or startup trying to grow your client base, a recruiter automating candidate outreach, or a freelancer looking for more clients, Waalaxy is one of the best HeyReach alternatives for you.

### **4. Dripify**

Dripify is a software tool that enables users to automate LinkedIn outreach. It allows you to create “drip” sequences of actions such as profile visits, connection requests, messages, and follow-ups. The goal is to scale your LinkedIn prospecting by automating repetitive tasks while still targeting leads and managing outreach campaigns.

Users upload or segment a set of LinkedIn contacts (or filter them via LinkedIn), then define a campaign flow (e.g., visit → connect → message → follow up), set time delays and quotas, and let the system run in the background.

**Pricing (per user/month, billed annually):**

* Basic: $39/month; 1 campaign, limited daily quotas
* Pro: $59/month; unlimited campaigns, dedicated inbox, CSV export
* Advanced: $79/month; multi-team management, lead tagging, step analytics
* Enterprise: Custom

> Check out the [detailed review of Dripify here](https://www.heyreach.io/blog/dripify-review)

#### **HeyReach Vs Dripify: a side-by-side comparison**

##### **1. Pricing**

While Dripify utilizes a per-seat pricing model, HeyReach employs a flat fee model, which facilitates scalability, especially for B2B sales agencies working with multiple clients. Additionally, Dripify could improve by adding more useful features to the lower pricing tier. If you want more features, you may need to subscribe to a higher-priced plan, and paying per user for these plans may not be cost-effective.

##### **2. Multi-account Management**

While Dripify supports multiple users and a unified inbox, you can’t merge multiple accounts into one campaign. HeyReach is built specifically for multiple account management. You can connect 10, 20, or even 100 LinkedIn accounts and manage all replies from a unified inbox. It also allows multi-account sender rotation, meaning one campaign can automatically distribute messages across accounts.

##### **3. Email Outreach**

While HeyReach is LinkedIn-specific, Dripify uses both LinkedIn and email; however, the email automation features of Dripify are not as extensive. I would recommend another tool, such as Lemlist, if you want a tool that pays as much attention to email as LinkedIn.

#### **Takeaway**

If you want ease of use, a solid interface, good analytics, and you’re okay focusing mostly on LinkedIn, Dripify is a strong choice, just be aware of cost and limitations as you scale.

### 5. La Growth Machine

La Growth Machine (LGM) is a cloud-based multichannel outreach platform that lets you combine LinkedIn, email, phone calls, SMS, and X (Twitter) in a single campaign sequence. It's particularly strong on CRM workflow integration — the Ultimate plan syncs sent messages and replies directly into HubSpot or Pipedrive and can trigger CRM workflows automatically.

LGM uses an "identity" model rather than per-user pricing: you pay per LinkedIn or email identity running campaigns, and team members can be added for free.

**Pricing:**

* Basic: €60/month; LinkedIn + email, 250 leads enriched/month
* Pro: €110/month; adds calls, 400 leads enriched/month, unified inbox, A/B testing, Clay/Zapier/Make
* Ultimate: €110/month; adds CRM sync (HubSpot/Pipedrive), CRM enrichment, workflow triggers
* Agency: Custom (6 identity minimum)

#### HeyReach vs. La Growth Machine: a side-by-side comparison

##### 1. Channel Breadth

LGM is the most channel-diverse tool on this list — LinkedIn, email, calls, SMS, and X in one sequence. If your outreach strategy spans multiple channels simultaneously, LGM gives you a single platform to manage it all.

##### 2. LinkedIn-Specific Features

HeyReach focuses exclusively on LinkedIn, which means its LinkedIn-specific capabilities are more developed: multi-account rotation, account safety, MCP integration, and flat-fee agency pricing are capabilities LGM doesn't match on the LinkedIn side.

##### 3. Pricing Model

LGM's per-identity pricing is competitive for smaller teams. For agencies managing 50+ LinkedIn accounts, HeyReach's Agency plan ($1,399/mo for 50 senders) offers more predictable costs.

##### 4. CRM Integration

LGM's Ultimate plan offers deeper CRM workflow integration — syncing messages and replies directly into HubSpot or Pipedrive and triggering CRM workflows — which goes beyond what HeyReach currently offers natively.

#### Takeaway

La Growth Machine is the strongest option for teams that need coordinated multichannel sequences with deep CRM workflow integration. If LinkedIn is your dominant channel and you're running it at agency scale, HeyReach is the more purpose-built solution.
