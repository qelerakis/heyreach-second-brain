# HeyReach + EmailBison integration

Source: https://www.heyreach.io/blog/emailbison-announcement

Summary: Leads falling through the cracks? HeyReach + EmailBison keeps LinkedIn and email in sync — auto-switch channels, trigger sequences, and close more replies.

We're excited to announce our third multichannel integration: [**HeyReach + EmailBison**.](https://www.heyreach.io/integration/emailbison) After  [Instantly](https://www.heyreach.io/integration/instantly) and [Smartlead](https://www.heyreach.io/integration/smartlead-ai), EmailBison joins the HeyReach ecosystem giving you seamless LinkedIn-to-email and email-to-LinkedIn workflows without the manual headaches.

Most outbound teams run LinkedIn and email separately. Different tools. Different workflows. Leads fall through the cracks.

**Now you can run both channels in sync:**

* **LinkedIn DM not getting replies?** → Auto-switch to email via EmailBison
* **Connection request pending 5+ days?** → Trigger email sequence automatically
* **Email getting ignored?** → Shift to LinkedIn and break through inbox noise
* **Need multiple touchpoints?** → Run LinkedIn + email simultaneously for higher response rates

No manual exports. No CSV uploads. No copy-pasting. Just automated multi-channel outreach.

We recorded walkthrough videos showing the integration in practice—setup, configuration, and real workflows you can steal.
