## The main challenges of scaling LinkedIn outreach

Using LinkedIn for your cold outreach for sales in B2B SaaS is, hands down, the best way to go about it if your target audience is present. If you’re in the construction industry, for example, you’re still better with cold calling probably.

But there are definitely some challenges you need to overcome when it comes to scaling your efforts, especially when done manually.

### Limited personalization at scale

Targets in sales are getting crazier every year. Even if you spend your whole work day personalizing a message template and sending it to leads, it’s gonna be pretty hard to reach those targets and get a good first impression at the same time.

When you include [follow-ups](https://www.heyreach.io/blog/follow-up-email-and-linkedin-message) and tracking responses, together with filling in your [sales CRM](https://www.softr.io/templates/sales-crm) to have an overview of your productivity metrics - at one point you’ve just going to opt for a generic template that you send to every lead. In most cases, you won’t target any pain point of your potential customer, and these direct messages will get you flagged for spam, you’ll be ignored, or even blocked.

### Manual labor costs

Let’s say that you really want to do everything manually, and team targets are increasing. The next logical step would be to hire more SDRs to be able to deliver, right?

Well, if you’re in the US, that’ll cost you [$80k on average per rep](https://www.glassdoor.com/Salaries/sales-development-representative-sdr-salary-SRCH_KO0,36.htm). Then there’s also the amount of time you’ll spend in hiring, onboarding, and training new hires, plus managing the extra workload until the team can handle all of it.

There’s a very fine line between what’s actually worth it and what's not, especially when you’re scaling your startup.

Slow response time = missed leads

Our CRO, Ilija, constantly repeats:  “In sales, speed either kills or saves the deal”. The same goes when it comes to outreach.

And let’s face it. When you’re handling outreach manually, it’s easy to miss a hot lead just because you didn’t reply fast enough.

By the time you follow up, they’ve already gone with a competitor who responded quicker.

### Higher conversion costs

When everything’s done manually, there are bound to be some mistakes. From [prospecting](https://www.heyreach.io/blog/linkedin-prospecting), and [qualifying lead](https://www.heyreach.io/blog/lead-qualification-process)s, to outreach – slow responses or human errors can lead to a lower conversion rate, and in the end, a higher conversion cost.

The more time you’ll have to spend on an outreach sequence out, the more it’ll cost to actually turn a lead into a customer. And if your messaging isn’t dialed in, you’re burning through leads without getting enough conversions to justify the effort.

If LinkedIn has so many challenges, why not simply go for something more traditional, like cold email outreach?

### But why LinkedIn and not cold email outreach?

Well, don’t get me wrong. Cold email works. But it comes with its own set of challenges.

Engagement plays a huge role in cold emails. So, if they’re not getting opened or are just being deleted, you won’t stand a chance in actually showing up to your leads’ inboxes.

Plus, once you account for deliverability issues, spam filters, and the sheer volume of emails people receive daily, it becomes harder to stand out.

Sure, LinkedIn has its issues, don’t get me wrong.

But, there, you’re reaching a pool of potential clients inside of a professional network, where people go every day to work, business and job opportunities.

People can see your LinkedIn profile, job title, company you’re working with and know that they’re chatting with a person. Who knows, they may even acknowledge a common ground on interest, shared connection, industry, school that you two went. Depending on how well you’ve done your homework before reaching out. 😉

There’s a social element that makes your outreach more organic. Plus, with the right approach, you can leverage LinkedIn’s algorithm to keep your profile visible to more potential leads.

But I’ll be honest with you. For all of this to work without you having to spend extra time and money, manual isn’t going to cut it. You’ll need the help of some [automation tools](https://www.heyreach.io/blog/best-linkedin-automation-tools).

## How to increase the scope of your cold messaging with automation tools

When you’re scaling your LinkedIn cold outreach, the key is to leverage automation tools without losing that human touch.

The trick is to create campaigns that mimic the way you would manually reach out to a prospect. Except now, it’s happening on autopilot.

### Using automation tools to simplify the entire lead generation process

Before you even think about sending a message, you need high-quality leads. And let’s be real. Sifting through LinkedIn manually is a nightmare.

That’s where tools like Clay, RB2B, and Trigify come in.

* Clay pulls data from multiple sources, enriches profiles, and helps you build highly targeted lead lists.
* RB2B identifies and verifies detailed information about website visitors so you can turn your anonymous traffic into actionable leads.
* Trigify tracks real-time buying signals and intent data, helping you reach out to leads when they’re most likely to respond.

By automating the lead sourcing and enrichment process, you’re making sure you’re reaching out to the right people without spending hours on manual research (and doubling your caffeine intake).

### Keeping a balance between automation and personalization

Here’s the thing. Automation can get your message in front of more people, but if it sounds robotic, it’s going straight to the “Ignore” pile.

The goal is to scale conversations that feel real. That means:

* Rotating accounts of team members  in your outreach campaigns so you can reach more people
* Using smart personalization, like mentioning a lead’s recent post,  shared LinkedIn connection, event(s) you both participated in, LinkedIn group you’re both engaged in… you get the picture.
* Automating follow-ups without losing the human touch.

## The best LinkedIn cold message templates – Real-life examples that nailed the first message and the follow-ups

Before we dive deep into these, I want to give my two cents.

I always go for a very simple formula, whether it’s an inMail message or a first contact after connecting:

> [Observation]
> [Question]

That’s it. 2 sentences, all about the other person. That’s how you get a conversation going.

And, of course, another personal favorite that’s actually almost always led to a response is:

> “Hi [name], just wanted to say hi and that it's great to connect with you :)
> (optional) [Question]”

The simpler a message is, the more likely it is for you to get a response. Hell, we’ve got consistent results when our follow-up is a simple waving emoji 👋

But, of course, I’ll let the numbers and our Outbound Outliers do the talking now.

### 1. GTM motion

**Messages:**

> Hey [FIRST NAME] :), I saw on your website that you work in [INDUSTRY]. Since you likely work regularly with [ICP], I’ve put together a short guide on how you can improve conversions with sales AI agents.<br><br>If you’re interested, just send me a 👍, and I’ll be happy to share it with you. Tim

> Don’t worry, I won’t spam you further 😅. I’m just sending you these two guides that can help you convert more [ICP]. How to create Sales Agents: [LINK 1] Sales triggers: [LINK 2].If you want we can set up a call and I can show you a personalized demo of the AI Sales Agent.

**Results**:

* Messages sent: 184
* Reply rate: 45%
* Meetings booked: 5

**Why it worked**: The campaign's success was due to hyper-relevant, hyper-targeted messaging that added value up front. Tim used personalized insights from LinkedIn profiles and company websites to craft messages tailored to each lead’s industry and ideal customer profile, leading with helpful resources before asking for a call or sale.
