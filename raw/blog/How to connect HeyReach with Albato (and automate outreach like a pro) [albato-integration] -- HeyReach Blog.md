# How to connect HeyReach with Albato (and automate outreach like a pro)

Source: https://www.heyreach.io/blog/albato-integration

Summary: Learn how to integrate HeyReach with Albato using API keys and webhooks. Automate your LinkedIn outreach and sync HeyReach with your full tech stack—without code.

If you’re juggling multiple tools for lead generation, enrichment, and outreach, it’s time to let automation do the heavy lifting.

With Albato integration and two-way synchronization, you can connect HeyReach to hundreds of other apps, trigger actions in real time, and automate your sales workflows end-to-end.

Let’s walk together through how to integrate HeyReach and Albato so your outreach gets faster, smarter, and more scalable—without any copy-pasting or dev work.

### **The building blocks: what’s what?!**

Let’s break down what each tool does:

**HeyReach** – Your main LinkedIn outreach engine. It automates connection requests, follow-ups, and manages multiple LinkedIn accounts from one dashboard—with safety and deliverability built in.

**Albato** – A powerful no-code automation platform that connects your apps using webhooks and APIs. Think of it as your command center for triggering workflows across tools like CRMs, email platforms, forms, and now, HeyReach.

## **Why Connect HeyReach and Albato?**

Because with Albato you can automate and streamline lead flow and follow-up actions based on **real-time triggers**.

You can:

* Automatically push leads into HeyReach campaigns
* Trigger actions based on form fills, CRM updates, or LinkedIn events
* Sync HeyReach with 600+ apps (think HubSpot, Airtable, Notion, Typeform… you name it)
* Automate workflows that include diverse triggers and actions and use different historical data
* Build smart multistep automations without writing a single line of code
* Create and ecosystem that works for you

## **Step 1: Create a Trigger in Albato**

1. Head to your **Albato dashboard** and go to the **Automation** tab.

1. Click **Create new automation**.
2. Click **Add a trigger** and choose **HeyReach** from the dropdown list of apps.

1. Pick the **event** that should trigger your automation (e.g. “New reply”, “Message sent”, “Connection request accepted”).

1. When prompted to connect your HeyReach account, click **Add a connection**.

1. Name your connection (optional) and paste in your **HeyReach API key** (we’ll show you how to get that next).

## **Step 2: Get Your HeyReach API Key**

1. Go to your **HeyReach dashboard**.

1. Navigate to the **Integrations** tab and click **Get API Key**.
2. If you haven’t generated one before, click **New API Key**.

1. Copy the key using the double-page icon.

🚨 Important: Keep this key secure. Don’t share it publicly.

## **Step 3: Plug the API Key into Albato**

1. Go back to Albato and paste your HeyReach API key into the connection window.
2. Click **Continue**.
3. Albato will generate a **Webhook URL** for you—copy it.

This webhook is the secret handshake between Albato and HeyReach. Time to link them up.

## **Step 4: Add the Webhook to HeyReach**

1. In HeyReach, head back to the **Integrations** tab.
2. Click on **View/Create Webhooks**.

1. Click **Create Webhook**.
2. In the popup:

   * Give your webhook a name.
   * Paste the **Webhook URL** from Albato.
   * Select the **campaigns** and **event types** that match what you chose in Albato.

> ⚠️ Double-check that your event types in HeyReach and Albato match—otherwise, the integration might not fire correctly.

Once done, click **Create Webhook**.

## **Step 5: Build the Rest of Your Automation**

Now that HeyReach is connected to Albato, you can define follow-up actions with any other apps—think of:

* Updating your CRM when someone replies
* Notifying your team in Slack
* Triggering emails when leads connect on LinkedIn
* Adding leads to a Google Sheet or Notion doc for tracking

You’ll see your complete automation flow inside Albato. From here, you can keep adding steps and fine-tune it based on your workflow.
