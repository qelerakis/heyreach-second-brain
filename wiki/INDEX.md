## How to read this

This brain is built entirely from HeyReach's **own published content**, so it is distilled with that
in mind:

- **Vendor claims are labeled.** HeyReach's "best / only / #1" statements are marked as HeyReach's
  claim, not neutral fact. Its flagship data — the recurring **96,051-campaign benchmark** — is
  first-party and self-reported.
- **Featured practitioners are attributed by name**, with their agency noted as a conflict of
  interest; their tactics and numbers are theirs, not generic best practice.
- **Every reply/acceptance/conversion figure is self-reported** and kept with its context. Nothing
  here is independently verified.
- **Cross-source discrepancies are preserved, not reconciled** — e.g. the connection-request limit
  (~100 vs ~200/week), the sender sweet spot (6–20 vs 6–27), the free-trial conversion (10→20% vs
  9→21%), and HeyReach's own pricing tiers (see [[heyreach-pricing]] for the full contradiction table).
- **Time-bound tactics are era-tagged** ("as of 2026") — LinkedIn's limits and outreach norms move fast.
- **A few headline numbers are title-only** (appear in a video/post title but are never substantiated);
  these are flagged and not recorded as results (see [[aggregate-creator-claims]]).

Full sourcing lives in `../raw/`; the build method and prompts are in `_build/`.
