## LinkedIn Follow-Up Message: A Signal-Based System (+ Examples)
- **source_type:** blog_post

- **strategies/playbooks:** Signal-based follow-up: over-following LOWERS reply rates because LinkedIn is a social space (your face/name in notifications), not an inbox. Generic "follow up every 3–5 days, 4–6 touches" ignores conversation state. THE 4-STATE MODEL — decide by asking "what state is this conversation in?": State 1 NOT ACCEPTED (request pending 7+ days, no profile view/engagement) = a non-event; do NOT bump. Let the step expire, add to exclude list, switch channels. Procedure: send connection -> pause 10 days -> if no acceptance, find email -> "Add to Instantly" (Instantly Campaigns) -> start email sequence (or "Instantly Lists" to collect for later); reverse works too (stalled cold email -> route to LinkedIn via profile view/contextual request). State 2 ACCEPTED, NO REPLY (first message unanswered after 5–7 days) = one follow-up then pause; cap campaign to 2–3 steps, 7+ day delay; tag "Accepted – No Reply" in Unibox, gather into a list (use as exclusion or a later campaign). State 3 ACTIVE, NO REPLY (they post/comment but ignore your message) = don't follow up immediately; wait for a NEW signal (posts about a relevant challenge, comments on a topic, changes roles, engages with your company) or a 3–4 week gap; tag "Active – No Reply," remove from active sequences via exclusion list. State 4 REPLIED ONCE THEN STALLED (one reply, no response 7+ days after YOUR last message) = warmest lead; send ONE thoughtful contextual follow-up referencing the last exchange (never "just checking in"); optional 15-sec voice note as a pattern-interrupt; then stop (no third nudge). States 1–2 = systematized/automated; States 3–4 = manual judgment, high-value only. FOLLOW-UP PRIORITY RULE: high-value (strong ICP, buying authority, strategic logo, engagement signals) -> manual contextual follow-up; medium-value -> structured automation (cap 2–3 steps, 7+ day delays, auto-tag, remove after limit); low-value/low-signal -> exit early, exclude, withdraw stale requests. WEEKLY WORKFLOW (~30 min): (1) filter Unibox for threads where you sent last + no reply 7+ days (5 min); (2) cut to 10–15 highest-value (5–10 min); (3) quick signal check — active? posted? role change? (10 min); (4) decide/tag/move on (5–10 min). Track ONE metric: reply rate on manual follow-ups; re-evaluate after 4 weeks.
