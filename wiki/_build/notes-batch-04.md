## These 4 LinkedIn Messages Booked Me 100+ Calls (Copy These)

- **strategies/playbooks:** Teaches "Message Market Fit" (MMF) and four message templates. MMF principles: (1) people are "single-issue voters" — they say yes for one primary reason, so test a single idea at a time, not five stacked benefits; (2) "tokenization is NOT personalization" — inserting `{{first name}}`/"I saw you sell to X" isn't experienced as personalization; cluster-level truths ("you focus on eating by macronutrient profile") resonate; (3) "deposit before withdrawal" (attributed to Josh Braun). Diagnostic method: anchor tests against the simplest campaign to "disprove the null hypothesis" before running crazier tests. FOUR MESSAGE TYPES: (1) OCCAM'S RAZOR — as simple as possible, passes the "spouse barbecue test" (explain like to someone outside your industry who barely cares); use as first message, sent either as a blank connection then message, or as the connection note itself. (2) OFFER-LED — extremely direct, lead with value (e.g. temporarily give product away free); isolates whether the concept fails vs whether it only works with a strong offer. (3) AGITATIONAL BUMP — a context-free follow-up that provokes a knee-jerk "what is this?" reaction and makes them scroll up and reread message 1 (play off Justin Michael's "thought bump"); adds "legs," doesn't add new info. (4) BREAKUP PITCH (softened) — give a deeper "why I reached out," soften the ask, signal it's the end, lead with empathy; formula: "asking because X, was hoping to show you why, would be great to hear back either way." Surfaces soft objections (e.g. "we just hired a new RevOps leader, reach out in a month" = a near-certain future booking; "we don't use LinkedIn" = clean disqualify).
- **frameworks/methods/tools:** "Message Market Fit" (vs Product Market Fit — "what they will say yes to given Y concept" when you have no product yet); Brian Balfour's "four quadrants of market fit" essays (origin). "Transferring ideas vs saying things" (what the audience HEARS is audience-focused and wins; what you SAY is self-focused and doesn't). "Avoid landmines" (forced phrases a persona would never use blow up the message). Diagnosing what makes campaigns work: TARGET MARKET (the set of companies + operational/situational truths, not just firmographic filters) → AUDIENCE ("the CEO of the problem," Justin Michael's phrase — who actually owns the problem, e.g. a senior VP RevOps vs the CRO) → IDEAS shared.
