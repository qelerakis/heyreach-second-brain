Founder's takeaway: "this system saved his life" — when growing fast you can't manually vet hundreds of profiles but can't afford wrong hires.
- **numbers/benchmarks:** 800 accepted connections + 571 replies in 2 weeks; scaled $10K → $160K MRR in five months (the growth that triggered the hiring crisis).
