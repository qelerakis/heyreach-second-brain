(8) Add an "out": end CTAs with "…or not, let me know" to remove tension / look less needy. (9) Use social proof SUBTLY — one micro-line ("had a client in this niche, got them to 2x ROAS, shall I show you?"), not a case-study link or testimonial video. (10) Inverse of 7: with a weak offer, DON'T pitch until you uncover pain (ask "what's going wrong / are your ads scaling?"). (11) NON-NEGOTIABLE: use Sales Navigator's "Posted on LinkedIn" filter to keep only actively-used profiles (can cut a 10K list to 2K) -> 2–4x higher connection acceptance. (13) Prioritize by public intent signals — liked/commented, profile-viewed, or connected -> up to 10x response rate reaching people already aware of you. (14) Keep testing; the 1st/10th/20th message may not land. (15) Goal of first message = get a REPLY, not book a meeting. (17) NON-NEGOTIABLE: always run connection campaigns via automation (HeyReach — upload lists, click go, live in 10 min) to max your weekly quota (LinkedIn's weekly quota does NOT bank/roll over). (18) If a convo dies, ask more questions — end statements with a question. (19) Qualify QUIETLY with proxy metrics ("how many new ad creatives do you launch monthly?" instead of ad spend). (20) Personalize beyond "Hey [first name]" — reference their website/YouTube/news and name a specific feature. (21) Delay links/decks/assets — DMs are for understanding pain, not sending resources. (22) Focus on THEM — use "you" more than "I/we" in the first message. (23) Be short & scannable — short separate lines, not paragraphs (unless an old-school industry treats LinkedIn like email). (24) Only a % of the market is in-market — be flexible, pivot to giving value if no pain. (25) Run more than two conversation tracks at once (feels natural, higher response). (26) Use simple one-liner replies ("What do you mean by that?") as a frame flip. (27) Intelligent follow-up: if ghosted after a good convo, wait 1–2 weeks and send just a compliment. (28) Track connection acceptance; aim 15–30% avg. (29) Send BLANK connection requests (~20% higher acceptance) — exception: an irresistible offer, then include the note. (30) Build trust first by posting content consistently on your personal profile (case studies, hot takes, insights) -> higher acceptance/response/meeting rates.
