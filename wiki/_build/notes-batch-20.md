## Multichannel outreach, done right
- **source_type:** product_page
- **source_url:** https://www.heyreach.io/multichannel-outreach
- **topics:** multichannel, email-outreach, linkedin-outreach, sequences-campaigns, automation-stack, integrations, deliverability-safety-bans, heyreach-product, client-reporting
- **strategies/playbooks:** Detailed multichannel playbook (HeyReach's own advice):
  • Warm up on LinkedIn first (connection requests, profile views, post engagement), then follow up via email once interest shown.
  • "Send leads from Smartlead to HeyReach" — no email reply → auto-send to HeyReach, follow up on LinkedIn.
  • "Route leads from HeyReach to Smartlead" — LinkedIn invite not accepted after **5 days**, OR connected-but-no-reply → auto-send to cold email tool.
  • Sequences should be **conditional and progressive, not linear**. "Steal this" structure: (1) Primary touch = LinkedIn connection request via HeyReach; (2) Conditional follow-up — if accepted → LinkedIn message, if ignored → email follow-up via Instantly/Smartlead after X days; (3) Cross-channel reinforcement referencing prior touch ("Sent you a note on LinkedIn—sharing more context here."); (4) Channel-specific pacing — LinkedIn: lower frequency + higher personalization; Email: structured follow-ups with value-based CTAs; (5) Single reply source of truth — a reply on either channel auto-pauses all steps everywhere.
  • Automation stack: LinkedIn via HeyReach (multi-account rotation, daily safety limits, centralized inbox) + Email via Instantly/Smartlead (smart inbox rotation, deliverability controls, opens/replies-based follow-ups) + status-based automation (reply on LinkedIn → pause email; reply on email → stop LinkedIn) + optional Make/Zapier layer (sync reply status, enrich leads, route booked meetings to CRM) + fail-safes (auto campaign pauses on replies, channel cooldowns to avoid over-touching).
- **frameworks/methods/tools:** native integrations Instantly, Smartlead, EmailBison (two-way lead-data sync); 'Find Email' step (built-in email enrichment, credit-based); Make / Zapier for workflow layer; HubSpot for reporting.
- **product/features:** native Instantly/Smartlead/EmailBison integrations; "Find Email" step to identify verified professional emails inside HeyReach ("Drag, drop, sync - no GTM engineer needed"); bidirectional sync so responses pause outreach on both platforms; HubSpot CRM tracking of sent messages, replies, booked meetings, revenue at activity/contact/company level.
- **opinions/hot-takes:** "This prevents the awkward scenario of messaging someone on LinkedIn while simultaneously emailing them the same pitch."; "Turn LinkedIn silence into email pipeline." (EmailBison tagline).
- **numbers/benchmarks:** Illustrative bar chart (December 2025): emails sent **100**, DMs sent **80**, DM replies **79**, email replies **38**, bookings shown. NOTE: 79 DM replies from 80 DMs sent is implausible → treat chart as illustrative/placeholder, NOT a real benchmark. "5 days" invite-not-accepted threshold; "wait 2 days" then find-email step (from diagram); "7,000+ companies"; G2 4.7.
- **people/companies:** integration vendors Instantly, Smartlead, EmailBison; Make, Zapier; HubSpot.
- **voice/quotes:** "Multichannel outreach, done right"; "No more compromises on the tools, manual exports, and clunky handoffs."; "Dead-simple by design, to keep you fast."
