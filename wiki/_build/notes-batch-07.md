## Email vs LinkedIn message: Which one should you choose?
- **source_type:** blog_post
- **source_url:** https://www.heyreach.io/blog/email-vs-linkedin-message
- **topics:** multichannel, email-outreach, linkedin-outreach, deliverability-safety-bans, targeting-icp-lists, sales-process, agency-operations, heyreach-product
- **strategies/playbooks:** Channel SEQUENCING framework (not "which channel is better?" but "which channel can absorb your mistakes / should lead based on ICP-validation stage, volume, and social cost of being wrong"). EMAIL-FIRST when: ICP is a hypothesis (untested), messaging untested (email tolerates rapid experimentation — "test five subject lines in a week"), you need volume/many touchpoints to see signal, social risk high if wrong, testing 3-4 sub-ICPs. Email diagnostics: hard bounces=bad list, spam complaints=bad message, "not interested"=wrong timing; "LinkedIn acceptance with no reply tells you nothing." LINKEDIN-FIRST when: ICP validated & narrow (already closed deals), relationships matter upfront (consultative/founder-led; legal/finance/M&A/exec coaching), volume deliberately low (20-50 decision-makers), brand/founder profile is an asset, high-ticket long cycles ($50K+ ACV named accounts). LINKEDIN-AS-FALLBACK/LATER: after email engagement (opened 3x no reply), after warm signals (site visit/content/webinar), after referral or trigger event (funding, job change), when LinkedIn is validation not discovery. Pre-launch checklist (4-5 questions, "eight minutes"): Q1 Is ICP validated (10+ replies from exact persona, consistent objections, booked meetings) or guessing? → testing = cold email mandatory. Q2 Does identity matter before relevance? (identity-first buyers check profile → LinkedIn; relevance-first → email). Q3 Need volume in week one? (200+ touchpoints = email leads; <50 named accounts = LinkedIn viable). Q4 Social cost if message is wrong? (email = private/forgettable failure; LinkedIn = public, "remember you as someone who didn't do their homework").
