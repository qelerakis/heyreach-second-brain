# HeyReach Certified Experts — Grouped Profiles

Profiles of the **16 agencies** listed on HeyReach's `/experts/` directory, grouped by what they actually sell. Every one of these pages is **self-authored marketing** (source_type: certified_expert) — thin, superlative-heavy, and light on verifiable metrics. HeyReach's "certification" is its own program; the profile copy is the agency's own. So read this as a positioning map, not an endorsement: where a page claims a benchmark, a ranking, or a client roster, it is a **vendor/self-reported claim** with a conflict of interest, and most pages carry no metrics at all. The most data-rich page is Practical Prospecting; the most concrete stacks are Top of Funnel (Clay + n8n + Instantly.ai) and Understory (Clay + HeyReach + Instantly). Several founders here also appear as featured practitioners in [[featured-outbound-practitioners]]. For the compact table of all 16 plus the practitioners, see [[experts-directory]].

## GTM engineering & RevOps systems
Agencies selling "systems," not campaigns — unifying outbound, inbound, CRM, and enrichment.

### Harochi
Builds GTM engines that unify outbound, inbound, CRM, and marketing into "one connected operating system," aimed at giving marketing, sales, and leadership a shared data foundation (source: "Harochi"). ICP: ambitious scaleups and large enterprises. Rejects treating outbound/inbound/CRM/marketing as separate functions — "companies do not just run campaigns. They build durable systems" (source: "Harochi"). No specific tools named and **no metrics** — thin page, promotional throughout.

### OneThousandPieces
Self-described "Certified GTM Engineers" covering the funnel "from TAM to pipeline": custom scraping, data enrichment, CRM cleaning, and outbound playbooks, with named motions **news-led outbound, social-signal targeting, website retargeting, and territory management** (source: "OneThousandPieces"). Integrates from Salesforce to HubSpot; client industries span SaaS, healthcare, finance, manufacturing. Social-proof figure: clients "have collectively raised over $900M in funding" — note this is **aggregate client funding, not an outreach result they produced** (source: "OneThousandPieces").

### Understory
An "allbound" agency combining outbound, **paid media (LinkedIn, Google, Meta, Reddit ads)**, and RevOps into "a unified growth flywheel where every channel feeds the next" (source: "Understory"). Names a concrete stack — **Clay, HeyReach, and Instantly** — and a team of **28** partnered with **100+** companies, listing clients Clay, HockeyStack, Expensify, RemoFirst, Worldpay, and RB2B (note Clay is both a listed tool and a listed client). Carries a superlative claim — "the most innovative, long-standing, and constantly evolving agency in the space" — and asserts many "modern outbound and attribution tactics...started as experiments in our client work" (source: "Understory"). Treat client logos and the superlative as unverified social proof.

### Workflows.io
Combines AI workflows with human expertise across three offerings — **Content Engines** (case studies, interviews, LinkedIn distribution), **Automated Outbound Systems** (email + LinkedIn, enrichment, personalization), and **AI RevOps Agents** (CRM hygiene, real-time qualification) — plus ABM, for B2B technology companies (source: "Workflows.io"). Pitch: replace "weak content, spammy outbound, messy RevOps, and wasteful ads" with "clear, automated systems." Only claim is vague — "generate millions in revenue opportunities"; no tools named, no hard figures (source: "Workflows.io").

## AI-led & signals-based outbound
Agencies leading with buying signals, AI personalization, and (mostly) Clay.

### SalesCaptain 🚢
Offers six named motions: **AI-Led Outbound, Inbound-Led Outbound, Signals-Based Outbound** (funding, hiring, tech adoption, leadership changes), **CRM Enrichment, AI-Powered RevOps,** and **Leads Reactivation & Recontacting** (source: "SalesCaptain 🚢"). Led by **Bill Stathopoulos**, the **Clay Chapter Lead for London** — so Clay is implied as the core enrichment layer. Headline claim: recognized as the **"#1 B2B Outbound agency by Clutch"** — a third-party-ranking claim, unverified here (source: "SalesCaptain 🚢").

### CMM
Done-for-you outbound built on "advanced AI and API-powered workflows that continuously adapt," aiming for repeatable pipelines over one-off campaigns (source: "CMM"). Claims to be certified in **Clay, Instantly, Octave, and HeyReach** — "the essential platforms driving today's most effective outbound strategies" — and, notably, "the world's only agency officially certified in Clay, Instantly, Octave. and Hey Reach" (source: "CMM"). Flag: **unverified superlative**; the "four essential platforms" framing is time-bound (2026); no metrics.

### Vertical GTM
An "AI-powered B2M growth engine" using **Clay-powered targeting, multi-channel outreach (email, LinkedIn, and X), and "bulletproof" deliverability**, with autopilot systems that track job changes, enrich data, and route every lead into the CRM "enriched, mapped, and ready to close" (source: "Vertical GTM"). Framed as performing "like a 50-person sales team without the overhead." Self-reported benchmarks: **98% inbox placement, reply rates 3x the industry average, 5x better lead-to-positive-reply ratios**, with founders who claim experience "scaling SaaS to nine figures" (source: "Vertical GTM"). All vendor-sourced.

### TAM Acceleration
Builds "AI-powered B2B revenue engines" combining outbound, LinkedIn prospecting, ABM, and RevOps into one system that "delivers meetings directly to your sales team while you sleep" (source: "TAM Acceleration"). Diagnoses a "broken" GTM — "a patchwork of tools, teams, and wasted revenue" — and promises to turn "outbound teams into closers instead of data clerks." Claims clients "generate millions in new revenue" and "achieve up to 97 percent ROI" — **unsubstantiated**; ends on a "book your strategy call" CTA (source: "TAM Acceleration").

## Email-first & deliverability outbound
Where LinkedIn is one channel and cold email + deliverability is the backbone.

### Practical Prospecting
The most metric-rich page in the certified set. A 3-step custom system to "book meetings on autopilot — in 90 days or less": **(1) Email Infrastructure** (domains/accounts + a **14-step deliverability checklist**), **(2) Precision Targeting** (**25+ data sources** to find "ready-to-buy" ICP leads), **(3) Practical Messaging** (proven frameworks + pain-focused copy + AI personalization) (source: "Practical Prospecting"). Cites a "$500k tech stack" and **1,000+ meetings** generated across dozens of industries. Self-reported client case studies: an online-learning platform — **75 meetings from 6,378 emails, 5.43% reply rate**; a staffing company — **234 meetings from 35,855 emails**; an enterprise SaaS platform — **35 meetings, 33% conversion rate from replies** (source: "Practical Prospecting"). Usable benchmarks, but vendor-sourced (COI); note this is primarily an email shop — HeyReach is not named in the body.

### Cymate
A B2B outbound agency that "takes ownership of your top of funnel" — building lead lists, writing copy "that resonates," and ensuring "bulletproof deliverability" — running targeted ("right buyers, at the right moment, with the right message") rather than volume campaigns (source: "Cymate"). ICP: B2B software companies and tech startups. Positions against alternatives: "Hiring an SDR is slow and expensive. Generic agencies overpromise and underdeliver" (source: "Cymate"). No named tools; deliverability/ROI claims are unsubstantiated; thin page.

### Top of Funnel
Designs "enterprise-grade" automation for the entire top of funnel — "architect ecosystems," not just campaigns — on an explicitly named stack: **Clay-powered data enrichment, n8n workflows, and Instantly.ai deliverability** (source: "Top of Funnel"). Promises "lead lists that build themselves," personalized outreach that "scales to thousands," and reporting, all running 24/7 "without multiplying your headcount." Signature line: "pipeline is oxygen. And we make sure you never run out" (source: "Top of Funnel"). Notable mainly for naming a concrete Clay + n8n + Instantly.ai stack; no hard metrics.

## Founder brand, content & fractional marketing

### Platinum Agency
Helps founders scale their business and LinkedIn presence via strategic content + AI-powered lead gen — content to attract ideal clients and position the founder as a thought leader, plus outreach systems that book qualified conversations (source: "Platinum Agency"). Two models: **done-for-you execution** or **done-with-you setup**; scope runs "from profile optimization to campaign design." Founder is **Sönke Venjacob**, who also recurs in HeyReach's testimonial roster (source: "Platinum Agency"; source: "Meet the HeyReach world"). Claim — founders going "from inconsistent lead flow to fully booked calendars in just weeks" — is vague and unsubstantiated.

### AMPLIS
Positions as "your flexible in-house marketing partner" — an alternative to a full-time hire or a traditional agency — offering fractional CMO support, outbound marketing, and fully managed marketing teams, adapting scope to "save time, reduce costs, and unlock sustainable growth" (source: "AMPLIS"). ICP: small/mid-size service businesses. Pitch is the "best of both worlds" (strategic leadership + hands-on execution). The thinnest page in the set — no metrics, tools, or named practitioners (source: "AMPLIS").

## Boutique test-and-scale & demand-gen agencies

### frontBrick
An AI + human demand-gen agency for high-growth B2B: AI-driven targeting, scalable outreach infrastructure, personalized email, LinkedIn, and **account-based (ABM)** campaigns, aiming for "a repeatable engine for growth" rather than "random tactics" (source: "frontBrick"). ICP: high-growth B2B in SaaS, consulting, digital services. Claims "Trusted by over 100+ B2B companies" and clients who "generate millions in new pipeline" — **unsubstantiated** (source: "frontBrick"). Founder **Stan Stojanovic** authored a HeyReach event-promotion playbook (see [[featured-outbound-practitioners]] and [[event-led-outreach-campaigns]]).

### The Deal Lab
Builds multichannel outbound programs "that prove what works in the market": validate ideas, create early momentum, then scale winners into "reliable growth engines" — a test-and-scale approach whose "edge is strategy and precision" (source: "The Deal Lab"). Founder is **Kellen Casebeer** (booking via a Calendly link on the page). No metrics — the value proposition is the methodology, not reported results (source: "The Deal Lab").

### ScaleMate
A boutique agency for B2B founders and revenue teams that blends **strategy, enablement, and automation** into "a repeatable growth engine," spanning outbound prospecting through inbound scaling and designed to grow with a client "whether you're validating product-market fit or pushing toward Series B" — "without the bloat of a large agency" (source: "ScaleMate"). Founder **Halen Youles** — "award-winning Account Executive," "30 under 30 ARN Tech Award recipient," and "first sales hire at CloudKitchens" (self-reported creds; no campaign metrics) (source: "ScaleMate").
