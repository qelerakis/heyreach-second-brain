# Featured Outbound Practitioners

The named people and agencies whose campaigns HeyReach publishes as "Outbound Outliers" playbooks, blog case studies, and videos — grouped by the motion they're known for. **Every number here is self-reported by the practitioner** about their own campaign, published on HeyReach's marketing surfaces; each practitioner has a conflict of interest (most sell an agency, tool, or consulting service). Sample sizes are often small, denominators are sometimes ambiguous, and a few figures are title-only or explicitly pending. Cite them as *claimed*, keep the who/ICP/stack context, and never present them as verified. This is the biography layer under the campaign write-ups in `case-studies/`; for the compact index and the certified agencies see [[experts-directory]] and [[heyreach-certified-experts]].

## Multichannel & ecommerce outbound

### Gunner Park — Brij (RevOps & Growth)
Runs an ecommerce/CPG multichannel workflow (LinkedIn + email) for Brij (brij.it), whose CEO is **Kait Stephens** — Gunner seeds the audience from Kait's LinkedIn engagers via **Trigify**, enriches and qualifies in **Clay** (ClayAgent + OpenAI), finds emails via Apollo → Clay waterfall, segments into CPG/Durable/Baby Goods for dynamic social proof, layers RB2B website-visitor signals, then runs **three parallel HeyReach campaigns** and cold email via **Smartlead** (source: "How Gunner from Brij uses multichannel outbound to hit a 60% reply rate"). Claimed **60% reply rate** — 90 messages, 42 accepted, 25 positive replies (source: "Perfecting your LinkedIn cold messages: Proven tactics for higher engagement and shorter sales cycles"; source: "How Gunner from Brij uses multichannel outbound to hit a 60% reply rate"). HeyReach's **Unibox** is heavily featured (he replies to everyone as Kait from one inbox). Gunner also recurs in HeyReach's testimonial roster. Client proof points (Chamberlain Coffee, Health-Ade) are Brij's, not campaign results. See [[multichannel-linkedin-email-campaigns]].

### Joel Wylie & Cameron Brown — Duplo (co-founders)
Duplo is a multichannel outbound agency. Their GTM-client workflow builds audiences with **Trigify + Clay** (filter C-levels/founders, enrich, GPT-qualify) then runs a 3-step HeyReach sequence off a "free leads" hook (empty connection request → value message + competitor leads 3h later → soft profile view + case-study nudge) (source: "LinkedIn lead generation strategy for 60%+ reply rates"). Claimed: **254 leads → 38% connection rate → 25% reply rate → 15 qualified calls in one week, 6 converted to customers** (source: "LinkedIn lead generation strategy for 60%+ reply rates"). A separate persona-matched Duplo campaign is credited with a **54% reply rate and 49 qualified leads** (source: "Persona-perfect LinkedIn outreach templates: Steal our ICP flows"). The "Duplo playbook" (reply classification/routing) is also cited by the agency **Repify**, which claims **6 retainers in 1 week** and **15 calls in 7 days** (source: "Sales handoff workflows: ready-to-run RevOps playbooks for lifecycle orchestration"). See [[multichannel-linkedin-email-campaigns]].

## Relationship-first & human outreach

### Steven Brady — GTM operator (misterbrady.com)
A "human-first" relationship play: monitor a specific thought leader with **Trigify** (his primary example is **Alex Boyd**, Co-Founder of Aware — chosen because Steven knows Alex personally *and* genuinely uses Aware), qualify ICP-fit in **Clay**, then send a hand-written, tag-free, non-AI message via HeyReach's "If Connected" branch, with a one-day social-proof follow-up (source: "How Steven closed $49,000 in revenue over 8 weeks"). Claimed: **$49,000 in revenue over 8 weeks** from 85 leads (45% accepted, 52% replied), with ~7% ICP-fit yield from a raw ~10,000-prospect list (source: "How Steven closed $49,000 in revenue over 8 weeks"). Caveat: the method leans on a *personal* relationship with Alex Boyd and authentic product usage — not replicable by anyone lacking that; small sample (85 leads). See [[relationship-first-and-human-outreach]].

### David Moreira / David Emanuel — Automate rev.ops (co-founder)
A Clay-workflow consultant who runs relationship-first outreach to **1st-degree connections** (like-before-message), pairing Clay with HeyReach's native integration (source: "From LinkedIn connections to clients: relationship-first approach"). Claimed: **32% reply rate, 100% meeting-to-reply — 36 messages, 12 replies, 12 booked** (source: "From LinkedIn connections to clients: relationship-first approach"; source: "Perfecting your LinkedIn cold messages: Proven tactics for higher engagement and shorter sales cycles"). Name discrepancy across HeyReach's own pages: one page names him **David Moreira / Automate rev.ops** (automaterevops.com), another **David Emanuel / AutomateRevOps** — same campaign, both preserved here (source: "From LinkedIn connections to clients: relationship-first approach"; source: "Perfecting your LinkedIn cold messages: Proven tactics for higher engagement and shorter sales cycles"). See [[relationship-first-and-human-outreach]].

### Mohan Muthoo (Mohan KM) — Spring Drive (founder)
Founder of Spring Drive (springdrive.co), a lead-gen agency. Runs a deliberately casual, "human approach": Sales Navigator (US, specific titles/seniority, recently active) → **Clay + Anthropic (Claude)** AI openers → two casual sentences + an open-ended question, A/B-testing the transition question (source: "How Mohan got a 40% reply rate using a human approach"). Claimed **40% reply rate**, but the denominator differs across HeyReach's pages: the playbook frames it as **293 replies from 2,192 leads** (≈13% of all leads — the ~39–40% is among a targeted/accepted subset), while the blog roundups report **730 messages → 291 replies (40%), 127 positive** (source: "How Mohan got a 40% reply rate using a human approach"; source: "LinkedIn lead generation strategy for 60%+ reply rates"; source: "Outbound automation tools: What works and what you’re probably doing wrong"). Cite the denominator carefully. See [[relationship-first-and-human-outreach]].

## Signal & intent-based outbound

### Gilbert Kralinger — Enablement.ch (COO)
COO of Enablement.ch, which helps sales teams scale revenue via AI/automation without hiring. His "4 golden rules" framework — targeting, timing, qualification, personalization — runs a heavy multi-tool stack: **Sales Navigator, Ocean.io, Bardeen, Apify** for targeting; **RB2B, HubSpot (job changes), Teamfluence** for intent timing; everything webhooked into **Clay** (Apollo + Clearbit enrichment, "ICP?" + "Persona fit?" gates); then LinkedIn via HeyReach and email via **Smartlead**, with AI translating signals into personalized copy (even into local languages) (source: "How Gilbert achieved 8% conversion rate in B2B outreach"). Claimed **8% conversion** vs a stated 1–3% "industry standard" — 136 messages, 12 replies, 10 positive (source: "How Gilbert achieved 8% conversion rate in B2B outreach"; source: "Perfecting your LinkedIn cold messages: Proven tactics for higher engagement and shorter sales cycles"). Caveat: "8% vs 1–3%" is a practitioner claim with no denominator shown for the comparison. See [[buying-and-intent-signal-campaigns]] and [[signal-based-outbound-framework]].

### Ashvin Jankee — Signado (founder)
Founder of Signado (signado.io: signal detection, ICP scoring, AI message generation), using his own tool to turn buying signals into LinkedIn conversations, with HeyReach as the send layer (source: "How Ashvin used buying signals to turn LinkedIn contacts into meetings"). Includes a strong first-person HeyReach endorsement (proxy infra, longevity — "obvious choice") = vendor-favorable testimonial. Claimed results are tiny and partly pending: batch 1 of **23 leads → 2 meetings**; **batch 2 (353 contacts) explicitly PENDING** — do not record as a result (source: "How Ashvin used buying signals to turn LinkedIn contacts into meetings"). COI: he's dogfooding his own product. See [[buying-and-intent-signal-campaigns]].

## Recruiting & candidate sourcing

### Nikola Sokolov — Influencers.Club (founder)
Founder of Influencers.Club (influencers.club, a creator-economy data provider). Adapted outbound tooling to *sourcing candidates*: pull 500–1,000 profiles per Sales Nav search → Clay AI scoring against a "living checklist" of red flags (tenure <18mo = −5, no SEO = −1, not growth/demand/product = −3) → LinkedIn outreach to over-threshold candidates → a **Make** automation that routes every reply into a pipeline with source, date, sentiment, and score (source: "800 Connections, 571 Replies, 2 Weeks: A Recruiting Masterclass"). Claimed **800 accepted connections + 571 replies in 2 weeks** (the hiring crunch followed scaling **$10K → $160K MRR in five months**) (source: "800 Connections, 571 Replies, 2 Weeks: A Recruiting Masterclass"). ## Agency operators running at scale
Featured HeyReach *customers* whose story is operational scale, not a single campaign's copy.

### Ramzi — Lux Sales Consulting
Operator at Lux Sales Consulting, an agency in the financial-services niche running an outbound lead-gen system on **50 LinkedIn profiles** inside HeyReach — centralized management and a master/unified inbox replacing 50+ separate logins, with its own CRM wired via HeyReach webhooks (source: "Lux Sales Consulting - An Agency managing 50+ LinkedIn profiles"). Context figures: company 75+ people, sales team 10+, grew from ~15–20 to ~40–60 accounts (expanding monthly), with dedicated IPs across 195+ countries/regions (source: "Lux Sales Consulting - An Agency managing 50+ LinkedIn profiles"). Testimonial COI (published on HeyReach's blog). See [[scale-linkedin-outreach-safely]] and [[agency-client-onboarding-and-reporting]].

### Linkunity — real-estate lead-gen agency
A lead-gen agency running Dubai/UK/Germany real-estate referral campaigns: connection request with an **empty note**, message #1 within 3 hours of acceptance, then a proof-heavy follow-up cadence, with seat rotation across 5 LinkedIn accounts and a centralized Unibox (source: "How Linkunity booked 55 meetings using LinkedIn outreach"; source: "Sales follow-up workflows: Speed-to-lead benchmarks & SLA templates that win deals"). Claimed **55 meetings, 26% reply rate, 36% connection acceptance** from the Dubai segment; an A/B test ("Book a 15-minute call this week?") reportedly **2x'd bookings** (source: "How Linkunity booked 55 meetings using LinkedIn outreach"; source: "Stop scaling too soon: a campaign audit framework that actually works"). See [[relationship-first-and-human-outreach]] and [[linkedin-follow-up-frameworks]].

### Rokketbase — performance SEO/web design for law firms
Muslim Zaidi (CEO) runs a warm-up-heavy sequence for a performance agency serving US/Canada law firms: Sales Nav targeting, then pre-connection profile view + engagement (only on content posted in the last 3 days) + follow, ~1h wait, A/B-tested connection request; accepted → auto visit → first message → BDR handoff in Unibox (source: "How Rokketbase booked 4 high-ticket meetings, in 23 days"). Claimed **4 high-ticket meetings in 23 days** (ACV withheld); agency context stats: 12M+ traffic generated, 67% average conversion rate (source: "How Rokketbase booked 4 high-ticket meetings, in 23 days"). See [[relationship-first-and-human-outreach]] and [[simple-and-direct-campaigns]].
