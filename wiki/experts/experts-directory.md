# Experts Directory — HeyReach's Certified Experts & Featured Practitioners

A "who's who" of the people and agencies HeyReach puts forward as authorities on LinkedIn/multichannel outbound: the **16 HeyReach-certified experts/agencies** (self-authored profile pages on heyreach.io/experts/*) plus the **notable practitioners** whose campaigns HeyReach features across its outbound playbooks, blog case studies, and videos. **Everything on this page is promotional and self-reported.** The certified-expert pages are marketing pages the agencies wrote about themselves; the practitioner numbers are results those practitioners reported about their own campaigns (each with a conflict of interest — most sell a competing or complementary service). Nothing here is independently verified. Treat every superlative ("world's only," "#1," "most innovative") as a vendor claim and every reply/acceptance/revenue figure as claimed, not proven. Use this as an index into the detailed profiles in [[heyreach-certified-experts]] and [[featured-outbound-practitioners]], and into the numbered campaigns in `case-studies/`.

## How to read this directory
- **Certified expert** = an agency that appears on HeyReach's `/experts/` directory with a self-written profile (source_type: certified_expert). Certification is HeyReach's own program; the copy is the agency's. See caveats in [[heyreach-certified-experts]].
- **Featured practitioner** = a named person/agency whose campaign HeyReach published as an "Outbound Outliers" playbook, a blog case study, or a video. Their numbers are self-reported — cross-referenced into `case-studies/`.
- **COI everywhere.** Several people appear in both lists (e.g. the founder of a certified agency who also authored a featured playbook), and some are simultaneously HeyReach *customers*, *integration partners*, and *testimonial faces*.
