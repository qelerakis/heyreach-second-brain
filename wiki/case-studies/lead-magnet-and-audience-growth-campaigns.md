# Lead-magnet & audience-growth campaigns

These campaigns use LinkedIn outreach not to book a meeting directly but to **distribute a value asset** — a narrow lead magnet, or a content hub — that builds credibility and an audience that converts later. The "ask" is genuinely helpful (here's a guide / can I feature your workflow?), which is what earns the reply. Both are on HeyReach's playbook library and run by their creators (COI noted). Watch the denominators: the headline numbers are partly tied to separate assets or are audience-growth (not sales-pipeline) metrics.

## Tim Yakubson (B2B Boosted) — lead-magnet distribution, 45%/26% best campaign

**Who:** **Tim Yakubson**, founder of **B2B Boosted** agency (b2b-boosted.com), who also sells a Clay course; part of the "Outbound Outliers" squad. The HeyReach author **discloses bias** ("I bought his course," "this isn't a paid promotion, I'm just that much of a fan") — so this is a COI on both sides (source: "Lead magnet outreach strategy [723+ qualified leads per month]"). This is the same practitioner as **Tim Jacobsen** elsewhere in the library — see [[event-led-outreach-campaigns]] and [[personalization-and-message-experiments]].

**Context & tactic:** Use LinkedIn outreach as the delivery channel for a lead magnet, targeting people who are problem- or solution-aware. Tim's strict rule: a lead magnet is **not** a brochure/deck that fixes a big problem — it's a **narrow niche problem-solver** that still requires you to hand-hold them through the rest (e.g. "profiles of remote MPC candidates + a savings calculator" for a recruitment agency; "a video on buying domains in under 5 minutes without manual DKIM/DNS" for a deliverability tool). LinkedIn campaign = connection request + **4 messages**: tease it ("Simply reply 'yes' and I will rush it to you") → send it anyway ("my bad, I couldn't hold back…") → "seen it? ^^" → "if you liked the last guide, you would love this one." Four follow-ups on purpose "because life happens" (source: "Lead magnet outreach strategy [723+ qualified leads per month]").

**Numbers (self-reported):** Typical **30–50% connection acceptance and 15–25% reply rates**; his **best campaign = 45% acceptance + 26% reply from 1,051 leads**; a **guaranteed minimum 5% reply rate** (he'll review a Loom if it fails) (source: "Lead magnet outreach strategy [723+ qualified leads per month]").

**Title-only caveat to preserve:** the **"723+ qualified leads per month"** in the title is tied to his **separate** lead-magnet blueprint, **not** substantiated as this playbook's measured result — the substantiated stats are the 30–50% / 15–25% (best 45%/26% from 1,051 leads) (source: "Lead magnet outreach strategy [723+ qualified leads per month]"). The 723/month figure recurs in a B2B Boosted video that derives it from **multi-sender volume math** (a 155-lead single-sender list yields ~93 qualified leads/month vs a 2,300-lead, 5-sender setup yielding ~220/month), crediting "team Jacobsen / B2B Boosted… 723 qualified leads every single month" (source: "I Found The EASIEST Way to Get Clients on LinkedIn in 2026") — i.e. the number is a portfolio/volume claim, and the sender-count mechanics belong in the frameworks/product notes on multi-sender rotation.

## Sam Classen (Startup Growth.ai) — 2,000+ newsletter subscribers in ~2 weeks

**Who:** **Sam Classen**, a growth marketer, co-founder of **startup cookie** (an AI-native agency) and founder of **Startup Growth.ai** (a content hub for growth marketers) — his properties are the COI (source: "How Sam Got 2,000+ Subscribers in 2 Weeks Using HeyReach").

**Context & tactic:** The goal is **newsletter/audience growth, not sales pipeline.** Startup Growth.ai is a single landing page of AI-powered GTM workflows, each with a downloadable implementation guide (a markdown file to drop into Claude Code, Codex, or Cursor); the trade is "drop your email, download the guide." A HeyReach campaign targets early-stage startup founders/operators (growth/marketing/AI/GTM interests) with a **genuine ask**: "I'm looking for AI marketing and go-to-market workflows that I can feature on Startup Growth.ai. Do you have any that you'd want to feature?" — people read it, visit the site, and subscribe. Two-branch HeyReach flow: if not connected, connection request → InMail on decline / standard message on accept; if already connected, a DM immediately. Follow-up 1 shares a live published workflow; follow-up 2 is a "Hail Mary." A Beehive/beehiiv signup form on the landing page auto-captures subscribers (source: "How Sam Got 2,000+ Subscribers in 2 Weeks Using HeyReach").

**Numbers (self-reported):** **~2,000 subscribers in 1–2 weeks; 25–30% newsletter open rate**; runs across multiple markets at once. **No connection/reply rate is given** (source: "How Sam Got 2,000+ Subscribers in 2 Weeks Using HeyReach").

**Their conclusion / limits:** "Most LinkedIn outreach is transactional… This campaign flips that by leading with a genuine ask" — the ask "works in two directions: it grows the audience and generates the content." **Use-case caution:** these are subscriber/open-rate figures, not sales pipeline; the "genuine ask" is effectively a lead-magnet hook.

## Cross-study note
Both lead with an asset and a genuinely useful ask rather than a pitch — the same value-first logic as [[relationship-first-and-human-outreach]], but the conversion event is a download/subscribe rather than a meeting. Many follow-ups (Tim's four; Sam's Hail Mary) are treated as helpful persistence, not spam.
