# Simple & direct campaigns (small stacks, blunt copy, sender rotation)

The counterweight to the heavy signal/enrichment stacks elsewhere in this library: these campaigns win with a **tight list, a short sequence, and direct copy** — sometimes no more than two messages, sometimes high volume spread across multiple sender accounts. The recurring lesson is that you don't need a complex tool stack to book meetings; a relevant list plus a blunt, outcome-first ask (and, for volume, sender rotation) is enough. All are self-reported and on HeyReach's own library/blog (COI noted per study).

## Luke Bivens (QC Growth) — $2.5m+ pipeline from a 2-message coffee hook

**Who:** **Luke Bivens**, founder of **QC Growth**, who ran this **for a client** — his agency is the COI, and the campaign was for an unnamed client (source: "Driving $2.5m+ in qualified pipeline with this LinkedIn campaign").

**Context & tactic:** After cold email, LinkedIn, and multichannel all under-performed for the client, Luke went LinkedIn-focused with a **simple 2-message** sequence targeting Customer Success leaders: (1) connection request with a personalized note using an **in-person hook** — "I'll be in NYC 1st week of Oct and wanted to reach out to a few CS leaders… open to coffee" — plus founder social proof and a one-line value prop; (2) on accept, a 1-day-later **auto profile-visit** to stay in notifications and "Who viewed my profile"; (3) the next day, a second message asking for feedback and re-proposing the Oct meetup (source: "Driving $2.5m+ in qualified pipeline with this LinkedIn campaign").

**Numbers (self-reported):** **359 connection requests → 49 conversations started → 17 qualified meetings → $2.5m+ in qualified pipeline.** Meetings were booked with leaders from **Microsoft, Slack, Sprinklr** (source: "Driving $2.5m+ in qualified pipeline with this LinkedIn campaign").

**Metric caveat to preserve:** "$2.5m+ in qualified **pipeline**" is pipeline, **not** closed/won revenue; and Microsoft/Slack/Sprinklr are **meetings with their leaders, not customers**. "A simple, but elegant campaign." (source: "Driving $2.5m+ in qualified pipeline with this LinkedIn campaign").

## Deepak (Craftgenn) — closed a $10k deal from 63 messages

**Who:** **Deepak**, founder of **Craftgenn** (craftgenn.com), which modernizes legacy systems and builds AI-driven automation — his company is the COI (source: "He closed a $10k deal with a simple LinkedIn outreach campaign").

**Context & tactic:** A deliberately minimal **2-step** campaign. Build the list in Sales Navigator (headcount, US region, function/specific titles, and **whether the company is hiring** — his heuristic: "nothing says 'ready to spend money' like a company already in spending mode"). HeyReach uses an "If connection" split: connected → message 1 immediately; not connected → connection request first, then message 1 on accept. Message 1 is a short qualifying ask; a 24-hour follow-up cites a proof point (source: "He closed a $10k deal with a simple LinkedIn outreach campaign").

**Numbers (self-reported):** Over 4 weeks: **12.7% reply rate out of 63 messages sent; 2 meetings booked; closed a $10k USD deal.** In-message proof point: "helped a startup reduce development and design costs by 60% while speeding up their product launch by 6 weeks" (source: "He closed a $10k deal with a simple LinkedIn outreach campaign").

**Limits:** Very small sample (63 messages / 2 meetings). The 60%-cost-cut / 6-weeks figure is an **in-copy client claim, not a campaign result.** The hiring-as-spend-signal insight also connects to [[buying-and-intent-signal-campaigns]].

## Patrick Thomason — 44% reply rate at volume with 6 senders

**Who:** **Patrick Thomason** (LinkedIn "patrickthomason"), who runs an "AI outbound system" booking qualified demos for B2B custom-dev-software companies; no agency named (source: "Turning LinkedIn outreach into a predictable B2B pipeline & deal flow").

**Context & tactic:** Minimalist but high-volume. Targeting uses **free LinkedIn Search** (not Sales Navigator) — copy/paste the search URL straight into HeyReach to import almost **4,000 leads**. Sending uses a **team of 6 senders** to spread volume without breaking LinkedIn limits. Campaign: connection request → if accepted, wait 6 hours → first message → up to 2 follow-ups; for those who ignore the request, like a recent post 24h later, then view their profile the next day. Copy is blunt and outcome-first — no pleasantries (source: "Turning LinkedIn outreach into a predictable B2B pipeline & deal flow").

**Numbers (self-reported):** **44% reply rate; almost 4,000 leads imported; 6 senders.** The message references booking qualified demos for "projects worth $5k-$10k on autopilot"; he claims "booked meetings that brought in thousands of dollars in just two weeks" (source: "Turning LinkedIn outreach into a predictable B2B pipeline & deal flow").

**Metric caveat to preserve:** the "44% reply rate" has **no stated denominator** (of the ~4,000 imported? of accepters?); "thousands of dollars in two weeks" is vague; "$5k-$10k projects" is in-copy, not a result. The 6-sender approach is framed as reaching more leads "without breaking any LinkedIn limits" — a HeyReach selling point (source: "Turning LinkedIn outreach into a predictable B2B pipeline & deal flow").

## Linkunity — 55 meetings in real estate with empty connection notes

**Who:** **Linkunity**, a lead-gen agency (HeyReach client), running a campaign for an unnamed **Dubai real-estate** client — Linkunity is the COI, and this is a HeyReach blog success story (source: "How Linkunity booked 55 meetings using LinkedIn outreach").

**Context & tactic:** A referral-partnership campaign (parallel similar campaigns in the UK & Germany, only local details differing). Sequence: connection request with an **empty connection message** ("best practice"), from optimized real LinkedIn profiles, with a **20-day** acceptance window then auto-removal; on accept, message #1 within **3 hours**; then follow-ups #1–#3 on a cadence, ending in a break-up message. Copy is direct and **proof-packed** (top-10 Dubai agency, 11,000+ listings, refer a buyer for 30% of commission paid within 7 days, an example partner payout of 1,700,000 AED). Runs across **5 LinkedIn accounts in rotation** with the Unibox for replies (source: "How Linkunity booked 55 meetings using LinkedIn outreach").

**Numbers (self-reported):** **55 meetings booked; 26% reply rate; 36% acceptance rate; 5 senders; 20-day acceptance window; first message within 3 hours** (Dubai campaign) (source: "How Linkunity booked 55 meetings using LinkedIn outreach").

**Caveats to preserve:** the tactical numbers shown are for the **Dubai campaign only** (UK/Germany called "equally successful" but unquantified); the in-message stats (11,000 listings, 30% commission, 1,700,000 AED) are the client's own **sales copy, not verified**. Being direct/sales-y works "when the audience fits and leads are well-researched" (source: "How Linkunity booked 55 meetings using LinkedIn outreach").

## Cross-study patterns
- **Empty connection notes** recur (Linkunity; also HeyReach's own campaigns) — see [[personalization-and-message-experiments]] for the blank-vs-noted data.
- **Sender rotation for volume** (Patrick's 6, Linkunity's 5) rather than pushing one account harder — the safe-scaling principle in the frameworks deliverability rules.
- **Direct, outcome-first copy** is the shared voice; contrast with the casual/value-first pole in [[relationship-first-and-human-outreach]].
