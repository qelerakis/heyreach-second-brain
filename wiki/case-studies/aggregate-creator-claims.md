# Aggregate creator claims (unaudited lifetime/portfolio revenue)

HeyReach's YouTube channel hosts guest "masterclasses" whose titles headline large dollar figures — $5M, $11M, $203,874.03. These are **self-reported, unaudited, aggregate** numbers: usually a creator's lifetime or portfolio total across themselves *and* their clients, over years, not a single documented campaign. Per the corpus's own caveat on title/hype numbers, they are recorded here **only as claims**, quarantined from the measured campaign results elsewhere in these case studies. The genuinely reusable content in these videos is method (frameworks) and opinion, not the headline figure — pointers are given below. Each creator runs an agency (COI) and the videos are HeyReach-sponsored.

## "Amon" — $5M+ over 5 years

**Who:** A presenter heard as **"Amon"** who runs a LinkedIn outreach agency (COI); HeyReach-sponsored (source: "How I made $5 Million with LinkedIn Outreach (1 Hour Masterclass)").

**The claim:** **$5M+ over 5 years** (him + clients) — stated in the title and asserted in-body, self-reported and unaudited. He also name-drops booked calls with brands (Reddit, Liquid Death, Headspace, Deutsche Bank, Morgan Stanley, etc.), all unverified/promotional (source: "How I made $5 Million with LinkedIn Outreach (1 Hour Masterclass)").

**Concrete numbers worth keeping (still self-reported):** a **healthy example account at 3,948 sent / 1,815 accepted (~46%)**; ~1,000 sent ≈ 300 accepted; a ~30% acceptance rule-of-thumb (Ecom ~20%, SaaS/agencies 35–45%); the **"posted in last 30 days" filter lifts acceptance 2–4×**; and, attributed to HeyReach's own data, **a blank connection request gets 27% acceptance vs 22% with text** (source: "How I made $5 Million with LinkedIn Outreach (1 Hour Masterclass)"). The blank-vs-noted figure is used in [[personalization-and-message-experiments]]. His methods (the "observation then question" message framework, the "micro offer" concept, offer = outcome + credibility + easy-to-buy) belong to the frameworks category. Era: says "in 2025."

## Why these are quarantined
- **Aggregate, not per-campaign:** each mixes the creator's own results with clients', over long periods — no denominator, no timeframe you can normalize.
- **Unaudited & sponsored:** self-reported on a HeyReach-sponsored channel by agency owners who sell these services.
- **The value is the method:** the frameworks and opinions categories carry the reusable parts; only the concrete, campaign-level sub-figures (e.g. Amon's 3,948/1,815/46%, blank-vs-noted 27%/22%) are safe to cite as data, and always as claimed.
