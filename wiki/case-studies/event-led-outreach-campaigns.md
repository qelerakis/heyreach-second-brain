# Event-led outreach campaigns

Three documented campaigns use an **event** as the reason to reach out — either to fill your own invite-only event, to host an exclusive dinner as a trust-building funnel, or to meet prospects at third-party conferences they already attend. The shared insight: an event gives a concrete, low-pressure, non-pitch reason to connect, and (in the first case) lets people convert via a registration link **without ever replying**. All are self-reported; two are DACH-region, in-person, high-effort models rather than scalable auto-sequences.

## Stan / frontBrick — invite-only SaaS events (registration link inside the connection note)

**Who:** **Stan (Strahinja Stojanovic)**, founder of **frontBrick** (frontbrick.io) — his agency is the COI. **Skale** is named as paying for the events' food/drinks (the sponsor/likely client brand) (source: "LinkedIn outreach for event promotion [27% reply rate]").

**Context & tactic:** Fill invite-only, in-person events for SaaS marketers in specific cities (Paris, Amsterdam). The clever core: put a **lu.ma registration link inside the personalized connection-request note** so people can register directly without replying, and drive urgency with scarcity ("we can only accept 10-12 SaaS marketing leaders"). List-build: Crunchbase (ICP by size/traffic) → Apollo (contacts) → Clay (clean titles/companies, waterfall email, AI attendee qualification + SaaS verification). HeyReach sequence: personalized connection request stating their city + the invite + scarcity + the link → on accept, a value-packed follow-up listing the agenda → for non-accepters, wait 5 days then a similar message via InMail → both branches end by liking a recent post (source: "LinkedIn outreach for event promotion [27% reply rate]").

**Numbers (self-reported):** **243 leads generated; 45 connection-request acceptances; 16 direct replies (DMs + InMails).** Event outcomes: **26 signups for Amsterdam + 12 for Paris = 38 registrations.** Seats capped at 10–12 per event; 5-day wait before the InMail branch (source: "LinkedIn outreach for event promotion [27% reply rate]").

**Metric caveat to preserve:** the "[27% reply rate]" headline does not cleanly reconcile with 16 replies from 243 leads (≈7%) or 16 of 45 accepted (≈36%) — **and because many attendees registered via the embedded lu.ma link without replying, "reply rate" undercounts true conversions.** The concrete, defensible outcomes are **45 accepts + 38 event signups**. Geo-specific (Paris/Amsterdam) (source: "LinkedIn outreach for event promotion [27% reply rate]").

## Tom Soto Schiller / Grundwerk Digital — a CFO dinner as a trust funnel

**Who:** **Tom Soto Schiller**, CEO of **Grundwerk Digital GmbH** (grundwerk.digital), which offers cold outreach as a service for the DACH region — his agency is the COI (source: "How to sell on LinkedIn and build trust from day 1").

**Context & tactic:** After cold consulting pitches flopped, he built an in-person trust funnel: round up CFOs → invite to a face-to-face lunch → deliver value/networking → only then soft-sell. Clay filtered leads to **Frankfurt**, added each prospect's office location + ZIP, and suggested an ideal restaurant near each office. HeyReach opened with three warm-up steps (view profile → like post → connection request) as a "double notification strategy"; messages began only after acceptance. Message 1 invited them to an exclusive "CFO Dinner with 12 CFOs from the Frankfurt area" and asked to meet beforehand ("I'm a bit old-school and prefer personal contact and a firm handshake"); message 2 proposed a specific restaurant + Google Maps link + credibility ("spoken with over 550 decision-makers in [CITY]… over the last 4 years", "You won't have to expect a sales pitch"); message 3 offered an alternative restaurant + his personal phone number (source: "How to sell on LinkedIn and build trust from day 1").

**Numbers (self-reported):** **134 total leads; 20% acceptance rate; 50% reply rate.** The event is framed as a CFO Dinner for 12 CFOs; credibility claim "over 550 decision-makers… over the last 4 years" (source: "How to sell on LinkedIn and build trust from day 1").

**Metric note / limits:** 20% acceptance is **low**, but 50% reply among accepters is high — a small, high-touch funnel, not a scalable auto-sequence. Small sample (134). **Era/geo:** DACH, Frankfurt-specific, messages originally in **German** (the author's own English translation) (source: "How to sell on LinkedIn and build trust from day 1").

## Tim Jacobsen (B2B Boosted) — pre-conference "are you at [event]?" outreach

**Who:** **Tim Jacobsen**, founder of **B2B Boosted** (implements Clay/Claude Code/n8n) and Unlock Courses — his agency + courses are the COI. (This is the same practitioner as "Tim Yakubson" in [[lead-magnet-and-audience-growth-campaigns]] — a caption/name variant.) (source: "I Built a Clay to HeyReach Pipeline That Books 12 Meetings Per Month (Full Breakdown)").

**Context & tactic:** He calls event-based outreach an "underrated tactic." A ChatGPT prompt lists the top ~10 events in the prospect's city over the next ~3 months that the ICP attends; the opener is "Hey [first name], are you at [event]? I'm going to be at that event all week — would you want to find 15 minutes to compare notes?" — no pitch, and even a "not attending" reply opens a thread. Lead data comes from Clay's free "find leads" people search, is qualified by Claygent (an AI web crawler with a sculpted prompt) and scored, then pushed to HeyReach via the native integration; the HeyReach campaign views the profile, waits 1–2 days, sends the connection request, optionally likes a post and (if the profile is open) sends an InMail, then sends the pre-event message on acceptance. Replies are worked in the Unibox (source: "I Built a Clay to HeyReach Pipeline That Books 12 Meetings Per Month (Full Breakdown)").

**Numbers (self-reported):** The title claims **"over 12 qualified meetings every single month,"** but the only substantiated result is a **client's 3-month campaign: 47 positive replies → 15 booked sales demos (~5 demos/month)** (source: "I Built a Clay to HeyReach Pipeline That Books 12 Meetings Per Month (Full Breakdown)").

**Title-vs-body caveat to preserve:** the "12/month" is an unverified title-level claim; the documented figure is 47 replies / 15 demos over 3 months for one client. Era: references GPT-5.1 (2026).
