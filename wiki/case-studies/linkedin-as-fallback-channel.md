# LinkedIn as the fallback channel (no-email salvage & inbox-warm-up stopgap)

Two playbooks position LinkedIn as the channel to use **when email can't do the job** — either because a prospect has no findable/valid email, or because new email domains are still in their 2–4 week warm-up window. Both are HeyReach's own content and lean heavily on HeyReach product claims (one's second message is essentially a HeyReach benefits pitch; the other offers free HeyReach seats), so read them as promotional. One's headline number is a title-only "potentially" claim with no supporting data.

## Michael — salvage prospects with no email (route them to LinkedIn)

**Who:** A practitioner identified only as **"Michael,"** apparently a lead-gen provider (thin attribution); the piece is HeyReach's own (source: "Find active LinkedIn prospects [50% more replies]").

**Context & tactic:** When a prospect has an invalid or missing email, don't disqualify — route them to LinkedIn. HeyReach + Clay identify LinkedIn prospects with **over 500 connections**, treating 500+ as a proxy that the prospect is actively building their network (and thus likely to see/respond). Process: trigger for prospects with invalid/missing emails → check for >500 connections → add qualified prospects to HeyReach. The sequence is a "one-two message punch": message 1 is a peer-to-peer opener ("I tried to email you but my email enricher couldn't find your address… you're offering lead gen services too, what do you do when the email is missing…"); message 2 pitches LinkedIn automation's advantages over email (source: "Find active LinkedIn prospects [50% more replies]").

**Numbers:** The title claims "**[50% more replies]**," but the body states only "potentially increasing replies by 50%" — a **"potentially" claim with no substantiating campaign data**, so it should **not** be recorded as a result. The only concrete number is the **500-connections targeting threshold** (source: "Find active LinkedIn prospects [50% more replies]").

**Caveats to preserve:** message 2 is a direct **HeyReach benefits pitch** — "As scalable as email," "No pay-per-seat," "Safe," "Don't get OOO," "No Deliverability problems," "You can start immediately (no warm-up)" — these are **vendor claims, not neutral fact** (source: "Find active LinkedIn prospects [50% more replies]").

## "First campaign for agencies" — 7 qualified leads while email warms up

**Who:** **HeyReach** (first-party). Third parties cited: **OneAway** (an outbound agency) and its team member **Xavier**; and event hosts Enzo, Alex, and Noah (source: "7 qualified leads from 33 connection requests").

**Context & tactic:** A way for lead-gen agencies to deliver early value during the **2–4 week window** while new email domains/inboxes warm up (when email can't send yet). HeyReach + Clay: import qualified leads from Clay → a "Lookup Single row" dedupe against existing contacts → GPT clean the name/title/company → start the LinkedIn campaign. The sequence front-loads **warm-up "notification" steps** — view profile, follow, like the most recent post — so your name pops up in notifications a few times **before** the connection request (run over two days); then branch on acceptance, with 3 follow-ups spaced 1 / 2 / 3 days (source: "7 qualified leads from 33 connection requests").

**Numbers (self-reported):** **7 qualified leads from 33 connection requests sent; 51.5% acceptance rate.** The offer in the copy = **10 free HeyReach seats for 30 days**. A cited third-party stat: Xavier (OneAway) contacting email OOO auto-responders on LinkedIn gave "10% more positive responses across 17 clients" (attributed to Xavier/OneAway, unverified) (source: "7 qualified leads from 33 connection requests").

**Their conclusion / limits:** Don't sit idle during inbox warm-up — run LinkedIn to bring clients early value; the notification nudges prime recognition before the request. "7 leads from 33 connection requests sent... I'd say this is a good day for a sales person." The premise depends on the email-warm-up constraint (era/deliverability), and the copy actively pitches HeyReach + free seats (source: "7 qualified leads from 33 connection requests").
