# Engagement-signal campaigns (targeting post & content engagers)

The most-repeated targeting motion across HeyReach's featured playbooks is to reach out to people who **engaged** with LinkedIn content — likes and comments on an influencer's posts, a competitor's posts, a client's posts, or a niche topic — rather than to a demographic Sales Navigator list. The thesis is that an engager has self-selected as interested, so acceptance and reply rates climb. The campaigns below are all documented on HeyReach's own "Outbound Outliers" library or its blog/YouTube channel, so treat every number as **self-reported by HeyReach or the featured practitioner**, usually run with the practitioner's own tool (a conflict of interest noted per study). Numbers are verbatim and never independently verified.

## Repify — 15 qualified sales calls in one week (Trigify engagement targeting)

**Who:** Joel Wylie & Cameron Brown, co-founders of **Repify** (tryrepify.ai), a multichannel outbound agency — their agency is the COI; the tactic and results are theirs, featured on HeyReach's playbook page after an intro from HeyReach's CRO Ilija (source: "15 qualified sales calls in a single week").

**Context & tactic:** Targeted via **Trigify** two ways — (1) people engaging with top GTM influencers (C-level/founders in B2B SaaS), and (2) broader topic-based engagement filtered by title/location/company size. Per-prospect **Clay** enrichment identified each prospect's case studies, sales rep, target customer, and top 3 competitors. The offer was an "Alex Hormozi move": **give away a hand-built list of leaders engaging with the prospect's competitors, for free** — leading with tangible value instead of a guide/webinar. HeyReach ran the sequence: empty connection request → if accepted, message 3 hours later → profile-view nudge + follow-up 1 day later → post-like + final message 2 days later (source: "15 qualified sales calls in a single week").

**Numbers (self-reported):** **15 qualified sales calls booked in 1 week; 6 closed on a retainer; 25% reply rate.** First message sent 3 hours after acceptance (source: "15 qualified sales calls in a single week").

**Their conclusion / limits:** Their biggest challenge as an agency was "cutting through the overwhelming noise in the outbound world"; the fix was leading with free value. Small, single-week sample; a promo teaser for an unreleased "Outbound Outliers" Clay-table product is attached to the page.

## HeyReach's own campaign — 54% reply rate, 49 qualified leads (Trigify + Clay)

**Who:** **HeyReach itself** (first-party), showcasing the native **Trigify ↔ HeyReach** integration — a double COI (vendor promoting its own campaign *and* an integration partner) (source: "54% reply rate and 49 qualified leads with this workflow").

**Context & tactic:** Used Trigify's "Social Signal" to scrape everyone who interacted with the last ~20 LinkedIn posts of influencer **Max Mitcham** → a 1000+ lead list. Clay added a do-not-contact check, a ChatGPT percentage fit-score against HeyReach's offer/ICP, and name-cleaning, then pushed to HeyReach: empty connection request → profile view → message referencing the prospect's `{interaction_type}` of Max Mitcham's post → follow-up revealing the Trigify integration → like a recent post and stop ("We don't want to spam people") (source: "54% reply rate and 49 qualified leads with this workflow").

**Numbers (self-reported):** **54% reply rate and 49 qualified leads**; "a positive response for every 3.5 leads we've contacted." A secondary in-copy figure: **101 leads in 7 days from 496 people contacted.** The video version of this campaign, published on HeyReach's channel, gives the message-level denominator: **86 replies from 159 messages sent (54%), 49 qualified leads** (source: "This LinkedIn System Booked Me 80 Qualified Sales Calls in 30 Days"). The page notes "we got a few additional replies and leads than when the video was recorded," which explains the two slightly different slices (source: "54% reply rate and 49 qualified leads with this workflow").

**Discrepancy to preserve:** the 54%/49 headline (159 messages / 86 replies) and the "101 leads in 7 days from 496 contacted" figure are different cuts of the same campaign — do not collapse them. The video's own title claim of "80 Qualified Sales Calls in 30 Days" is a self-plus-clients aggregate that is *not* broken out; the only hard-verified workflow stat is the 86/159/54%/49 set (source: "This LinkedIn System Booked Me 80 Qualified Sales Calls in 30 Days").

## Aidan Buckley — 58% reply rate on AI-SEO post engagers

**Who:** **Aidan Buckley** (LinkedIn handle "aidan-buckley-outreach"); no company/tool named, so the COI is thin (just the HeyReach-page promotion) (source: "How a simple campaign achieved a 58% reply rate").

**Context & tactic:** Found 5–6 posts about "AI SEO," exported the engagers into HeyReach. Sequence: like a post → wait 3 hours → connection request **with** an attached message referencing their AI/SEO engagement → if accepted, continue manually; if not accepted, view profile + send an **InMail** 3 hours later. The whole design qualifies leads through conversation rather than an immediate pitch (source: "How a simple campaign achieved a 58% reply rate").

**Numbers (self-reported):** **58% reply rate; 295 people "actually talk to him"; the InMail step alone generated 47 replies** (source: "How a simple campaign achieved a 58% reply rate").

**Their conclusion / limits:** The 58% happened "because he actually tried having conversations instead of immediately pitching," and because engagement targeting gives "something in common to talk about." Era-bound to "AI SEO" as the shared topic; relies on InMail + LinkedIn message limits (free vs Sales Navigator).

## Sönke Venjacob (Platinum Agency) — 48% reply rate from a client's engagers

**Who:** **Sönke Venjacob**, founder of **Platinum Agency**, who now sells this engagement-to-meeting system as a productized service — a COI (source: "Smart way to turn your LinkedIn engagers into a lead gen machine").

**Context & tactic:** Built on a **client's** LinkedIn content engagement (a warm, not cold, audience). **Phantombuster** scraped everyone who liked/commented into a **Clay** table that filtered already-contacted leads and ran an account-fit check. The standout move was **segmentation by comment topic** (cold email? GPT templates? LinkedIn DMs?), routing each topic to a different, topic-relevant HeyReach campaign. Cadence: connection request → +2 days like a post → +3h first message → +4 days follow-up, casual and value-first ("Forgot to mention I made free resources…") (source: "Smart way to turn your LinkedIn engagers into a lead gen machine").

**Numbers (self-reported):** **174 total leads; 57% acceptance rate; 48% reply rate** (source: "Smart way to turn your LinkedIn engagers into a lead gen machine").

**Their conclusion / limits:** "If you're creating content on LinkedIn and getting engagement, you're sitting on a goldmine of warm leads." The results are from a warm engagement audience, not cold; uses the third-party scraper Phantombuster.

## Steven Brady — $49,000 in 8 weeks from thought-leader engagers

**Who:** **Steven Brady**, a GTM operator (misterbrady.com). He monitors thought leaders he *genuinely* follows — chiefly **Alex Boyd** (co-founder of **Aware**) — because he personally uses Aware and can open conversations authentically with its users. Method is not replicable by anyone lacking that authentic connection (source: "How Steven closed $49,000 in revenue over 8 weeks").

**Context & tactic:** **Trigify** monitors the chosen thought leaders and imports engagers with the interaction type + comment text; **Clay** runs an ICP-check prompt (employee count 9–50,000; a defined country whitelist) and pushes ICP-fit leads to HeyReach via the native integration. HeyReach uses an "If Connected" split: connected leads get an instant, hand-written, tag-free message ("I've been a heavy user of Aware for ~6 months now…"); if no reply, a 1-day follow-up with a social-proof link. He renamed "AI" to "Authentic Interactions" (source: "How Steven closed $49,000 in revenue over 8 weeks").

**Numbers (self-reported):** **$49,000 in revenue over 8 weeks. From 85 total leads, 45% accepted connection requests and 52% replied.** From a typical ~10,000-prospect list he finds **~718 ICP-fit leads (~7%)**, described as "standard for targeted prospecting" (source: "How Steven closed $49,000 in revenue over 8 weeks").

**Cross-source corroboration:** The same $49K/8-weeks figure is repeated in two HeyReach blog posts — one adds **Clay** to the stack (HeyReach + Trigify + Clay) and frames it around SDR pain points (source: "Solving the real pain behind the LinkedIn outreach") — and the other lists it as "$49,000 in 8 weeks via HeyReach + Trigify" inside a buying-signals guide (source: "Outbound ICP signals: How to spot high-intent leads before your competitors"). The figure is Steven Brady's self-reported testimonial in all three; the stack description drifts (with/without Clay), which is worth preserving.

**Era note:** The corroborating blog observes that cold LinkedIn reply rates "fell from ~10–15% to ~5–7%" as the playbook got crowded — context for why engagement/signal targeting is pushed (source: "Solving the real pain behind the LinkedIn outreach").

## Cross-study patterns

- **Empty vs noted connection requests:** Repify and HeyReach's own campaign both open with an *empty* connection request; Aidan Buckley attaches a note. See [[personalization-and-message-experiments]] for the blank-vs-noted data.
- **Anti-spam close:** several campaigns deliberately "like a post and move on" rather than over-message.
- **Warm ≠ cold:** Sönke's and HeyReach's results are from warm engagement audiences; read acceptance/reply rates against that.
