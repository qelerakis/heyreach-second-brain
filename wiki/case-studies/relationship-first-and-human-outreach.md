# Relationship-first & human-tone campaigns

These campaigns win on **who you already have a warm path to** and on a deliberately non-salesy, human tone — mining 1st-degree connections, leading with free value, opening with curiosity or casual humor, and never pitching on the first message. All three are on HeyReach's playbook library, each run by a practitioner with their own agency/tool (COI noted). Because the audiences are warm (existing connections or active users), the acceptance/reply rates run high — read them against that, not against cold benchmarks. Numbers are self-reported.

## Tim Scheuer (Prospera.ai) — 45% reply rate leading with value

**Who:** **Tim Scheuer**, Co-Founder of **Prospera.ai** (prospera-ai.com), an AI agent for multichannel follow-ups — his tool is the COI (source: "45% reply rate with this sharp GTM motion").

**Context & tactic:** He prospected inside his **own 1st-degree connections** in Sales Navigator (segmented by company size + job title), then narrowed to the **DACH region** only. Prospeo exported the list to Clay, where AI research prompts built two merge variables per lead — an "Industry model" (a plain sentence of what they do) and an "Ideal ICP" (who their customers are). Message 1 referenced their industry and likely ICP, offered a short guide on improving conversions with sales AI agents, and asked for a 👍 to opt in. The follow-up sent two guides plus a personalized demo offer (source: "45% reply rate with this sharp GTM motion").

**Numbers (self-reported):** **45% reply rate from 184 total messages sent** (source: "45% reply rate with this sharp GTM motion").

**Their conclusion / limits:** "Leading with value, not pitches" — build credibility with genuinely helpful resources before asking. **Era/geo:** targeting was scoped to the DACH region and his **own 1st-degree network**, so the result is not from cold audiences.

## David Moreira (Automate rev.ops) — 32% reply rate, relationship-first

**Who:** **David Moreira**, Co-Founder of **Automate rev.ops** (automaterevops.com), a Clay-workflow/RevOps consultancy — his consulting business is the COI (source: "From LinkedIn connections to clients: relationship-first approach").

**Context & tactic:** Mines his **own 1st-degree connections**. Clay runs the qualification pipeline: Prospeo for contact/job/location data → an AI geo filter (keep only EU, USA, Canada, Dubai, Israel) → data cleaning (strip emojis/middle names, standardize titles) → AI title-qualification (include "SDR"/"BDR", exclude anything with "operations" or "HR") → a "Blacklist Clay" column to remove existing clients. Qualified prospects push to HeyReach via the native integration. Sequence: **like** the prospect's recent post → 3 hours later, a low-pressure curiosity opener ("are you aware of {TRENDING TOPIC}?") → replies handed to an SDR → if silent, a 3-day nudge that is **literally just ":)"** → a final message 5 days later (source: "From LinkedIn connections to clients: relationship-first approach").

**Numbers (self-reported):** **32% reply rate**, with a high (unquantified) reply-to-meeting conversion (source: "From LinkedIn connections to clients: relationship-first approach").

**Their conclusion / limits:** Low-pressure by design — the smiley nudge "reminds without pressure." The sequence is "versatile across industries"; results are from a warm 1st-degree audience.

## Mohan KM (Spring Drive) — 40% reply rate with a casual, human approach

**Who:** **Mohan (Mohan KM)**, founder of **Spring Drive** (springdrive.co), a B2B lead-gen agency — his agency is the COI. Copy is generated with **Anthropic/Claude** (source: "How Mohan got a 40% reply rate using a human approach").

**Context & tactic:** LinkedIn (unlike email) rewards authentic networking. He refined the list in Sales Navigator (USA, specific titles + seniority) and — the finishing touch — used the **"Posted on LinkedIn" toggle** to keep only active users. In Clay, Claude generated **two extremely casual sentences per lead**: sentence 1 = a casual compliment finding common ground ("your influencer marketing game is so cool"), sentence 2 = self-deprecating humor ("honestly, I'm so clueless about that stuff lol"). Template = `[SENTENCE 1] / [SENTENCE 2] / "what kind of clients do you go for?"`. He used HeyReach's **A/B testing** to trial three different transition questions (source: "How Mohan got a 40% reply rate using a human approach").

**Numbers (self-reported):** **39% connection acceptance rate; 40% reply rate;** "over half of those replies were eager prospects ready to talk business"; **293 replies from 2,192 total leads** in the list (source: "How Mohan got a 40% reply rate using a human approach").

**Metric caveat to preserve:** the 39%/40% rates do **not** reconcile with 293/2,192 (≈13%) as a share of the full list — the 39%/40% are almost certainly rates among a targeted/accepted subset, not of the 2,192 total. Cite the denominator carefully (source: "How Mohan got a 40% reply rate using a human approach").

## Cross-study pattern
All three keep the **first message pitch-free** and open with value, curiosity, or a casual compliment; the ask comes later. Two of three (Tim Scheuer, David Moreira) source from **1st-degree connections** — the warmest possible list — and Mohan filters hard for **active** users. The "Posted on LinkedIn" active-user filter recurs across the library (see also [[event-led-outreach-campaigns]] and the frameworks targeting notes).
