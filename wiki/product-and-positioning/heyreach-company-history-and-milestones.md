# HeyReach: Company History and Milestones

This is HeyReach's own account of its founding, growth, and milestones — assembled from its about page, a founder's AppSumo retrospective, webinar blurbs, and the CEO's public letter. **Every financial and traction figure here is self-reported by HeyReach** (no third-party audit), and several conflict across sources, so they are presented as claims with their era attached. HeyReach describes itself as founded in **2023** as "the first agency-friendly LinkedIn outreach tool" — an unverified vendor origin claim (source: "Meet the HeyReach world").

## Origin and early growth (~2023)

- Built after ~2 years in the outreach market because "no tool addressed agencies/sales-team lead gen"; positioned as a "next-generation LinkedIn outreach tool built for agencies and teams" (source: "How we made $200,000 in sales, in just 4 days").
- **Beta** launched end of February (free, no paying customers); **paid** version end of March; scaled to **~$10K MRR in 77 days** with a 4-person sales team, using "sales-led growth" — "selling HeyReach using HeyReach" (source: "How we made $200,000 in sales, in just 4 days").
- Company was ~15 people and actively fundraising at the time of that retrospective (early HeyReach era) (source: "How we made $200,000 in sales, in just 4 days").

## The AppSumo lifetime-deal launch: "$200,000 in 4 days" (~2023)

HeyReach's breakout milestone was its **AppSumo lifetime-deal (LTD) launch on SumoDay**, where it became the best-selling tool on the platform in 4 days (source: "How we made $200,000 in sales, in just 4 days"):

| Metric (self-reported) | Figure |
|---|---|
| Total sales | $190,080 (≈ "$200,000 in licenses") |
| Net revenue (after 50-50 AppSumo share) | $95,040 |
| Licenses sold | 1,117 (1,100+ accounts → 3,000+ LinkedIn seats) |
| Refunds | 34 (3% churn) |
| Highest-tier share | 95%+ of licenses |
| Conversion rate | 9.01% |
| Reviews | 33 five-taco reviews |
| Expected vs actual | expected ~500 sales; SumoDay ran 3x AppSumo's normal traction |

The intro to AppSumo came from Vedran Rasic (founder of LeadDelta) in Dec 2022; the deal was a 50-50 revenue share (source: "How we made $200,000 in sales, in just 4 days"). Founder's takeaway: use AppSumo "as a way to get a strong sign of early product-market fit," not primarily for money — "there's a life after AppSumo." **Era note:** these are *early-stage historical* figures (~2023), not current scale.

## The ARR story (self-reported, conflicting)

- CEO Nick's letter: "We just crossed **$13M ARR**, adding **$1M-1.5M+ ARR per month** In Q1," serving customers "for the last 3 years" (source: "Quick update from Nick (CEO @HeyReach)").
- Webinar blurb: HeyReach "grew from **$10M to $17M ARR in 8 months at 60% margin**," and estimates its own mistakes "probably cost us another $2-3M" (source: "HeyReach webinars").

These two do not cleanly reconcile ($13M "just crossed" vs $17M reached "in 8 months") — likely different points in time; present both as claims, not a settled figure. Company-age markers also drift across sources: "a little bit over two years" (self-review), "almost three years" (Campaign API), and "the last 3 years" (CEO letter) (source: "HeyReach Review: The scalable LinkedIn outreach tool for agencies & sales teams"; source: "Introducing HeyReach Campaign API"; source: "Quick update from Nick (CEO @HeyReach)").

## The 2026 LinkedIn page-removal disclosure

CEO Nick's letter discloses that **LinkedIn removed HeyReach's company page plus a few executive profiles** (CTO, CRO, CMO) — dated to the company's Q1/~3-years-in period (2026 era) (source: "Quick update from Nick (CEO @HeyReach)"). HeyReach's framing (read as **vendor claims**, not verified fact):

- "This has zero impact on you or the product" — automations kept running, accounts "completely safe," "Zero technical impact."
- "LinkedIn can't detect who uses HeyReach."
- LinkedIn "has done this to every major tool in the space" — naming Apollo, Seamless, Evaboot, Lemlist, and La Growth Machine — and "None of them skipped a beat."
- The restrictions are "temporary," and HeyReach expects to be back on LinkedIn eventually; in the meantime it pivoted its own presence to X (@HeyReach_io).
- Ethics reaffirmation: "No fake accounts get created. No shady shortcuts. No data misusage. Ever."

This is a self-serving PR framing of a LinkedIn enforcement action; the "zero impact," "can't detect," and safety claims are not independently verified. The deliverability and ban-avoidance debate belongs to the opinions category; the safety mechanics to [[safe-linkedin-sending-limits]].

## Culture and leadership

HeyReach lists **6 operating principles** — (1) Be a smooth operator, (2) Clear targets, clearer deadlines, (3) Stay curious, (4) Always be kind, (5) Fail loud & do it fast, (6) Little things matter — and a brand ethos of "quality over quantity" (source: "Meet the HeyReach world"). Named leadership across sources: **Nikola Velkovski ("Nick," CEO)**, **Vukašin Vukosavljević ("Vuk," CMO)**, **Ilija Stojkovski (CRO)**, and **Nadja Komnenić (Head of Sales)** (source: "Quick update from Nick (CEO @HeyReach)"; source: "HeyReach webinars"; source: "HeyReach Review: The scalable LinkedIn outreach tool for agencies & sales teams").
