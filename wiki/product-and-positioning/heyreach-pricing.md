# HeyReach: Pricing and the Flat-Fee Wedge

HeyReach's pricing is its primary positioning weapon: it charges **per LinkedIn sender/seat, never per user**, so unlimited teammates, VAs, and clients can be onboarded "completely free of charge" (source: "Unlimited senders, one flat monthly fee"). Against competitors it frames this as flat-fee-for-many-senders versus per-seat pricing, and presents rivals' multi-sender tiers as unavailable except via "Custom deals" (source: "HeyReach comparisons"). **Important caveat:** the exact tier prices, sender counts, and billing discounts *self-contradict across HeyReach's own posts* — the ranges and the conflict table below are the reliable takeaway, not any single figure. All figures are 2026-era and, as HeyReach itself notes, "changes fast."

## The pricing model

- **Charged per LinkedIn sender/seat, not per user:** "We just charge you per sender/seat" and "HeyReach charges only per LinkedIn senders, not per users" (source: "Unlimited senders, one flat monthly fee"). Admins/members who only manage replies, campaigns, or reporting (without connecting a sending account) are **not** paid seats (source: "Best LinkedIn Outreach Software in 2026 ❘ HeyReach Review ❘ Full Tutorial").
- **No per-action credits for outreach:** messages, InMails, connections, and activities are not charged as credits (source: "HeyReach Review: The scalable LinkedIn outreach tool for agencies & sales teams"). Separate **enrichment credits** fund the "Find Email" step, allocated per sender by tier.
- **Dedicated residential proxy** is included on the entry tier and bring-your-own on higher tiers (source: "Unlimited senders, one flat monthly fee").

At 10+ accounts the per-seat price is also quoted as dropping from **$79 to $59** per sender (source: "I reviewed and ranked 40+ best LinkedIn automation tools of 2026"; source: "HeyReach comparisons").

## Trials and special programs

- **Trials:** 14-day trial on Growth (3 LinkedIn accounts, no credit card); a 30-day trial is offered for Agency & Unlimited (request via form) (source: "Unlimited senders, one flat monthly fee").
- **Early-stage program** — heavily discounted; qualify if under **$250,000 ARR, fewer than 5 people, and not yet a customer** (source: "Unlimited senders, one flat monthly fee").
- **Done-for-you (VIP)** — fully managed: "You focus on closing. We handle everything else," with a dedicated GTM Engineer and Inbox Manager (source: "Unlimited senders, one flat monthly fee"). See [[heyreach-positioning-by-segment]] for the concierge/white-glove services bundled at each tier.
- **Certified HeyReach Expert / affiliate program** — pays **up to 40% affiliate revenue** plus co-marketing and qualified-lead handoff; program members' endorsements are therefore a COI (source: "HeyReach for lead gen agencies"). See the experts category.
