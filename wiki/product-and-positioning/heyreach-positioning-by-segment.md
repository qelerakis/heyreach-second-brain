# HeyReach: Positioning by Segment

HeyReach runs three dedicated landing pages — **for lead-gen agencies, for sales teams, and for growth teams / GTM engineers** — that repackage the same core product (unlimited senders, Unibox, workspaces, integrations) around each buyer's job-to-be-done (source: "HeyReach for lead gen agencies"; source: "HeyReach for sales teams"; source: "HeyReach for growth teams"). All three are HeyReach's own marketing, so the value propositions and the recurring testimonial roster are vendor claims and partner endorsements (COI), not neutral evidence. Each page carries the same "starting at $799/month" anchor, G2 4.7, and "2026 Fastest Growing Product" badge (source: "HeyReach for growth teams").

## For lead-gen agencies

The agency pitch: "Connect unlimited LinkedIn accounts and manage all replies in a unified inbox … all for one fixed cost" (source: "HeyReach for lead gen agencies"). Emphasis:

- **Per-client workspaces** — "Isolated data, controlled access, clean ops"; "Onboard a new client in minutes, not days" (source: "HeyReach for lead gen agencies").
- **Full white-label** — "Your logo, your colors, your domain, your sign-in page. Clients see your product, not ours," multi-brand supported (source: "HeyReach for lead gen agencies").
- **Dedicated residential proxy per account "that never rotates IP addresses"** — a deliverability/safety selling point (source: "HeyReach for lead gen agencies").
- **Volume thesis:** "More senders = more replies = more meetings booked" (source: "HeyReach for lead gen agencies").
- **Certified HeyReach Expert program** — up to 40% affiliate revenue, co-marketing, and qualified-lead handoff; featured partner agencies include Alex Fine (Understory, billed as "#1 allbound agency in the world"), Sönke Venjacob (Platinum Agency), Macklin Buckler (Harochi), and Sean Wilson (Vertical GTM) — all paid partners (COI) (source: "HeyReach for lead gen agencies").

See [[start-and-scale-a-lead-gen-agency]] and [[agency-client-onboarding-and-reporting]] for the operational playbooks, and the experts category for the partner directory.

## For sales teams

Positioned as a "predictable meeting engine" where "Your team moves as one — and leadership always knows what's working" (source: "HeyReach for sales teams"). Emphasis:

- **Shared team workspace** — full visibility, shared campaigns, shared inbox, team reporting.
- **Unibox collaboration** — reply on behalf of teammates, take over any conversation, send desktop voice notes, while a "keep personal messages private" toggle shields personal DMs (source: "HeyReach for sales teams").
- **Native HubSpot CRM sync ("Verified by HubSpot itself")** — every connection, message, and reply lands in the CRM as a contact activity for "real ROI visibility" (source: "HeyReach for sales teams"). "If it's in your stack, HeyReach talks to it."
- **Broader import sources on this page** — adds Apollo, ZoomInfo, and CommonRoom alongside Sales Navigator, Clay, and HubSpot (source: "HeyReach for sales teams").
- No performance metrics are substantiated on the page; the featured voices are John Roche (Intercom) and Zvonimir Beslic (ContactOut) (source: "HeyReach for sales teams").

See [[book-meetings-on-linkedin]] and the sales-focused playbooks.

## For growth teams / GTM engineers

The growth pitch: "Plug LinkedIn infrastructure into your GTM stack" and run "signal-based and AI-powered outbound" (source: "HeyReach for growth teams"). Emphasis:

- **Signal-based outbound** — capture intent signals (job changes, post engagement, website visits) and import qualified leads instantly: "Warm outreach hits differently than cold. The reply rates prove it" (source: "HeyReach for growth teams").
- **Stack-native** — native Clay, HubSpot, Make, N8N, plus multichannel email (Instantly, Smartlead, EmailBison) and **HeyReach MCP → Claude** (source: "HeyReach for growth teams").
- **Attribution** — real-time dashboards, reply-sentiment analysis, auto-tagged warm leads, filter by sender/campaign/team for "clean attribution" (source: "HeyReach for growth teams").
- **Speed claim** — "From lead list to live campaign in under sixty seconds" (unquantified vendor claim) (source: "HeyReach for growth teams").

See [[signal-based-outreach]] and [[build-targeted-lead-lists]].

## Concierge / white-glove services (all segments)

Across pages, HeyReach bundles human services: **white-glove onboarding** (setting up sender accounts, migrating data), a **dedicated GTM specialist** for integrating other tools, and **escalation support** — "Remember that time you tried assembling IKEA furniture without instructions? Yeah, we won't let that happen here" (source: "HeyReach for growth teams"). The fully-managed **Done-for-you (VIP)** tier adds a dedicated GTM Engineer and Inbox Manager (source: "Unlimited senders, one flat monthly fee"). See [[heyreach-pricing]] for what is bundled at each tier.
