# HeyReach: Product Overview

HeyReach is a cloud-based LinkedIn (and multichannel) outreach automation platform positioned for **agencies, sales teams, growth marketers, and GTM engineers** who want to scale LinkedIn outbound "without losing that much-needed personal touch" (source: "10x your LinkedIn outbound. Unlimited senders, one fixed cost"). Its signature commercial wedge is **unlimited LinkedIn senders under one flat monthly fee with automatic sender rotation** — charged per LinkedIn sender/seat rather than per user (source: "Unlimited senders, one flat monthly fee"; source: "HeyReach comparisons"). Everything on this page is HeyReach's own positioning: its self-reviews, landing pages, and CEO communications, so claims like "unmatched," "#1," and "the only tool" should be read as **vendor claims**, not independent fact (source: "HeyReach Review: The scalable LinkedIn outreach tool for agencies & sales teams"). HeyReach displays a **G2 rating of 4.7** and a **"2026 Fastest Growing Product"** badge across its site (source: "Unlimited senders, one flat monthly fee"; source: "HeyReach comparisons").

## What HeyReach is

HeyReach describes itself as a "next-generation LinkedIn outreach tool built for agencies and teams," created because "no tool addressed agencies/sales-team lead gen" when it launched (source: "How we made $200,000 in sales, in just 4 days"). Its self-review calls it "unmatched on the market ... when we speak about LinkedIn outreach tools" and "#1 LinkedIn messaging automation tool" (source: "HeyReach Review: The scalable LinkedIn outreach tool for agencies & sales teams"). The core loop, as shown in its own demo: connect one or more LinkedIn accounts, import qualified leads (Sales Navigator, LinkedIn free search, groups, events, posts, CSV, or native integrations), build a multi-step conditional sequence, let senders auto-rotate, and manage every reply centrally in the **Unibox** unified inbox (source: "10x Your LinkedIn Outbound With HeyReach [Demo Video]"; source: "Best LinkedIn Outreach Software in 2026 ❘ HeyReach Review ❘ Full Tutorial"). See [[heyreach-features]] for the full feature catalog.

## The core positioning wedge

HeyReach's central differentiator is pricing structure, not features alone: it charges **per LinkedIn sender/seat, never per user**, so an agency can "onboard your whole organization … completely free of charge" — unlimited clients, teammates, and VAs at no extra cost (source: "Unlimited senders, one flat monthly fee"). It frames competitors as per-seat tools whose multi-sender tiers are only available as "Custom deals," implying HeyReach is the one with published flat multi-sender pricing (source: "HeyReach comparisons"). The volume thesis behind it: "More senders = more replies = more meetings booked" (source: "HeyReach for lead gen agencies"). See [[heyreach-pricing]] and [[heyreach-vs-competitors]] for detail and the pricing contradictions.

## Who it is for

HeyReach maintains dedicated positioning for three segments (detailed in [[heyreach-positioning-by-segment]]):

- **Lead-gen agencies** — flat fee for unlimited accounts, per-client workspaces, white-label, dedicated residential proxies (source: "HeyReach for lead gen agencies").
- **Sales teams** — a shared "predictable meeting engine": one workspace, shared campaigns, shared inbox, team reporting, native CRM sync (source: "HeyReach for sales teams").
- **Growth teams / GTM engineers** — "Plug LinkedIn infrastructure into your GTM stack," signal-based and AI-powered outbound (source: "HeyReach for growth teams").

Its stated mission is "to create a simple yet effective way for agencies, salespeople, growth marketers, GTM engineers … to start genuine conversation with people on LinkedIn" (source: "Meet the HeyReach world").

## Brand philosophy: quality over quantity

HeyReach positions its outbound philosophy as "sending fewer messages in total, but starting more relevant conversations. Because quantity without quality is just… well, spam" (source: "Meet the HeyReach world"). It also operates a **BYOT ("Bring Your Own Tools")** integration philosophy — "It's your outreach party, we're just providing the venue" (source: "Meet the HeyReach world"). Its self-review draws an explicit line on what the product will *not* do: it is "not made for dirty gamers" — you "can't spam, burn accounts, or rent profiles," and it "will never ever let you automate AI generated comments on posts. Period." (source: "HeyReach Review: The scalable LinkedIn outreach tool for agencies & sales teams"). This anti-spam stance is HeyReach's stated brand position, not a benchmark; see [[safe-linkedin-sending-limits]] and the opinions category for the deliverability and AI-SDR debates.

## What ships as product surface

Beyond the app UI, HeyReach positions three programmatic/AI surfaces as first-class product: the **HeyReach MCP server** (free), the **HeyReach CLI**, and the **Campaign API** — "You describe the play, Claude runs it for you" (source: "Run LinkedIn outreach without leaving Claude, Chat GPT or OpenClaw"; source: "HeyReach CLI is here 🎉"; source: "Introducing HeyReach Campaign API").
